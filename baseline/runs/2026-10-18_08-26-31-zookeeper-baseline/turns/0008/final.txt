[FileTxnLog.getLastLoggedZxid](/home/nitro/Desktop/consensus-assurance/baseline/runs/2026-10-18_08-26-31-zookeeper-baseline/work/zookeeper-server/src/main/java/org/apache/zookeeper/server/persistence/FileTxnLog.java:365) initializes its result from the newest log filename. AssertionError: empty tail made getLastLoggedZxid report 2 instead of 1

This is election-relevant because `QuorumPeer` uses `getLastLoggedZxid()` when constructing its initial vote.
