F8’s reproducer is [t60_quit_leader_replication.rs](/home/nitro/Desktop/consensus-assurance/baseline/runs/2026-10-18_11-44-44-openraft-baseline/work/tests/tests/audit/t60_quit_leader_replication.rs). It keeps a chunked snapshot from node 0 to a learner in flight, partitions the new leader from that learner, and forces node 1 to become leader. Even after node 0 becomes a Follower, its old snapshot task continues:

```
deposed leader 0 sent 228 InstallSnapshot chunks after step-down; sample=[(0, 2), (0, 2), ...]
```

`Command::QuitLeader` is documented as “No longer a leader. Clean up leader’s data.”, but its runtime arm only clears `leader_data`; the `replications` map and the in-flight snapshot task remain. This is a role/cleanup and resource defect rather than a demonstrated safety violation.
