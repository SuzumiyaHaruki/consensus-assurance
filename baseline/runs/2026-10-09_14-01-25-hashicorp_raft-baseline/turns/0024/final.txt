Deliverable summary:

The one concrete defect found is in the shipped test helper `cluster.EnsureSamePeers` (`testing.go:691`). It reads its reference peer set once (`peerSet := c.getConfiguration(c.rafts[0])`) outside the retry loop, while `otherSet` is re-read on every poll. Since `EnsureSame` only compares FSM *command* logs and configuration entries never enter those logs, `EnsureSame` does not wait for a committed config entry to propagate; if `c.rafts[0]` lags at that instant, `peerSet` is stale forever and the helper fails after its 5 s deadline on a fully consistent cluster.

Evidence: three shipped tests flake with the identical `peer mismatch` signature — `TestRaft_ProtocolVersion_Upgrade_2_3` (~6/20), `TestRaft_HasExistingState` (4/20), `TestRaft_ProtocolVersion_Upgrade_1_2` (2/15) — while a refresh-each-poll control passes 20/20, a goroutine dump at the failure shows all nodes converged, a timing study shows node 0 stale at the first read 15% of the time with otherwise microsecond-scale lag, and applying the one-line refresh in a scratch copy drops both re-tested cases to 0/20. So the missing re-assignment is necessary and sufficient.
