The audit now has 21 confirmed findings. The latest addition is a reconfiguration-state bug:

- **A second reconfiguration returns `Ok(())` while the first is only buffered.** With no leader elected, the first StopSign is stored in `buffered_stopsign`; `accepted_reconfiguration()` does not count that as pending. A second valid reconfiguration therefore also returns success, but `forward_stopsign` silently discards it.
