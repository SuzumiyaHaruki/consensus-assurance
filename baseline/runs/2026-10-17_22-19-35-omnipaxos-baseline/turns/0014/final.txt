The audit now has 19 confirmed findings. This pass added two runtime API defects:

- **`AppendError::Timeout` is not retry-safe.** When `Assigned` replies are lost, a forwarded proposal can already be accepted and decided. Retrying after the documented timeout produced two identical entries in the decided log: [audit_timeout_retry.rs](/home/nitro/Desktop/consensus-assurance/baseline/runs/2026-10-17_22-19-35-omnipaxos-baseline/work/omnipaxos_runtime/tests/audit_timeout_retry.rs).
- **Dropped decided subscribers retain their capacity slot while the log is idle.** Closed subscribers are only reaped when a new decision triggers a push. With one allowed subscriber, dropping `rx_a` and subscribing `rx_b` left `rx_b` immediately closed: [audit_subscriber_reuse.rs](/home/nitro/Desktop/consensus-assurance/baseline/runs/2026-10-17_22-19-35-omnipaxos-baseline/work/omnipaxos_runtime/tests/audit_subscriber_reuse.rs).
