**F13 — quiesce turns a leader failure into an indefinite write outage** (main result)

`handleReadIndex` and `handleConfigChange` record activity via `n.qs.record(...)`; `handleProposals` does not. While quiesced, `Peer.QuiescedTick` only advances `electionTick` and never checks `timeForElection`, so after the leader of an idle cluster dies the survivors neither wake on a write nor campaign — every retried write is forwarded to the dead leader and times out indefinitely. A read (or config change) does wake the cluster, which is why the symptom is writes-only.

Reproduced with two new live tests in [zz_audit_quiesce_test.go](/home/nitro/Desktop/consensus-assurance/baseline/runs/2026-10-17_20-19-30-dragonboat_raft-baseline/work/zz_audit_quiesce_test.go): a passing characterisation (nodes log `entered quiesce`, then `exited from quiesce, msg type ReadIndex`, read and subsequent write both succeed) and a failing leader-loss test (`cluster did not recover after losing quiesced leader 1: timeout`). Adding `n.qs.record(pb.Propose)` in `handleProposals` makes the survivor exit quiesce, campaign and win; the same test then passes in `audit/diag-fixes/`.
