**New this pass**

- **Randomised multi-node protocol fuzz** (`TestAuditRaftSafetyFuzz`): 3-node and 5-node clusters, 3000 ticks each, 20% message loss, 0–2 tick reordering, and a 60-tick partition every 150 ticks. Per run it produces ~700 proposals, ~20 leader terms and ~650 committed entries, checking every 10 ticks that no two nodes are leader in the same term, that all committed entries agree on term and payload across nodes, and that per-node commit indexes never regress. After healing the network it requires a leader and a newly committed proposal. 20/20 runs pass — no safety or liveness violation.

- **Crash-safety of votes** (`TestAuditVoteSurvivesRestart`): drives a node into candidate state, persists `pb.State` through `Peer.GetUpdate`/`Peer.Commit`, relaunches it from the same LogDB, verifies term/vote are restored, and that a `RequestVote` from another node in the same term is rejected. Passes.
