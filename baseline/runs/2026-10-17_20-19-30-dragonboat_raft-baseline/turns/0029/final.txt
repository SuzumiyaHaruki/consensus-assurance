**Finding 4 (new, executed, minor): the exported channel transport factory cannot be used**

`plugin/chan/chan.go` exports `ChanTransportFactory` implementing `config.TransportFactory` for test use. Its `Create` produces a working `ChanTransport`, but `Validate` panics ("not suppose to be called") — and `NodeHostConfig.Validate` calls exactly that validator for `RaftAddress` (`config/config.go:551-554` via `GetRaftAddressValidator`), so `NewNodeHost` with this factory panics before anything can start:

```

--- FAIL: TestAuditChanTransportFactoryIsUsable
```

Upstream's own tests work around it by defining their own factory with a real `Validate` (`nodehost_test.go:770-780`), which confirms the transport itself is fine once wrapped. A one-line fix (`return len(addr) > 0`) in the fix-verification copy makes the test pass.
