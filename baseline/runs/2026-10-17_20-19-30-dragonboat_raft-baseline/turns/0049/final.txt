I picked up the peer-message trust boundary again and found a cheaper variant of the same failure class.

**F11 — one forged `Heartbeat` kills a follower**

The follower's `handleHeartbeatMessage` passes the leader's `Commit` straight into `entryLog.commitTo`, which panics when the value is beyond `lastIndex()`. The Replicate path clamps (`commitTo(min(lastIdx, m.Commit))`); the heartbeat path never did. A `Heartbeat` needs no anchor index and no log-term match — only `Term >= r.term` and a known `From` — so a single frame is enough, and the same shape is sent by an ordinary leader whose match tracking is ahead of a follower's actual log.
