This pass added the last Raft safety property to the fuzz — Leader Completeness.

**What was added**

The 3/5-node fuzz now also verifies, at every observation point: a leader in term T holds every entry from an *earlier* term that any node has ever committed. It maintains a global map of committed entries (with per-node watermarks to avoid re-scanning) and checks every leader against it.

My first version of the check also demanded entries from the leader's own or newer terms, and it tripped on a legitimately *stale* leader (term 13 leader still holding term-13 at index 107 while a term-14 leader had superseded it) — normal Raft behaviour, not a defect. After correcting the check to the standard property (entries from earlier terms only), all 20 runs pass, in addition to leader uniqueness, log matching, commit monotonicity and vote uniqueness.

So the fuzz now covers: leader uniqueness per term, log matching among committed entries, commit-index monotonicity, no vote change within a term, leader self-vote, Leader Completeness, and liveness after healing.
