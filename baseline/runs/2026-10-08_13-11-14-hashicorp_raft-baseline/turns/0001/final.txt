I audited the artifact and confirmed two things, both documented in [report.md](/home/nitro/Desktop/consensus-assurance/baseline/runs/2026-10-08_13-11-14-hashicorp_raft-baseline/work/report.md).

**Identity:** the source is byte-for-byte identical to upstream `github.com/hashicorp/raft` v1.7.3 (the tag whose commit hash is the run's `source_identity`). Whole-tree `diff -rq` against the pristine module cache shows zero content differences (`audit/verification-facts.txt`). So this is a clean baseline with no planted mutation, and I found no defect in the core agreement logic after reading the `appendEntries`, `requestVote`/`requestPreVote`, `checkLeaderLease`, `installSnapshot`, `restoreUserSnapshot`, and replication paths.

**Finding 1 (concrete, reproduced):** `(*cluster).EnsureSamePeers` in `testing.go:692-715` captures `c.rafts[0]`'s configuration once before the retry loop and never refreshes it, while re-reading the other nodes every pass. If that first read catches `rafts[0]` one step behind a just-committed configuration change, the comparison can never converge and the test fails after `longstopTimeout`. This makes `TestRaft_ProtocolVersion_Upgrade_1_2` and `_2_3` fail nondeterministically (~3/12 here), always as `{2-server config} != {3-server config}`.

I ruled out the alternative explanation ("the implementation fails to propagate the configuration") with a diagnostic test `audit_diag_test.go`: in 3/8 runs the immediate capture was already stale, yet all three nodes live-reported the new 3-server configuration within ~20 ms — i.e. Raft converges, the helper's frozen `peerSet` is the defect. Logs: `audit/upgrade-repeat.log` and `audit/audit-diag.log`.

Supporting executed checks: `go build ./...` and `go vet .` clean; `go test -race -short -run TestRaft_` showed no data races. All other full-suite failures were `listen tcp ...: socket: operation not permitted` (the sandbox blocks loopback), not logic failures.

Unresolved/not covered: the network transport and the nested `fuzzy`/`raft-compat` modules (the latter can't build offline — its `raft-boltdb` deps aren't cached). Diagnostic additions are confined to `work/audit_diag_test.go` and `work/audit/`; `source/` was never modified.
