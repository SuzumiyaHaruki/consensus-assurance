**New executed evidence — committed-entry churn soak**

`TestAudit_CommittedEntriesSurviveChurn` (`audit/soak.log`) spins up a 5-node in-memory cluster and, repeatedly, drives commands while isolating the current leader (forcing re-elections) and then healing it. It records every command for which `Apply` returned nil (i.e. committed) and afterwards asserts each one is present on every replica — a disappearing committed entry would violate Raft's core safety property. Result: **2/2 runs passed**, 64 committed entries over 8 churn rounds per run, every replica retaining every committed entry.

That is meaningful negative evidence for leader-election/log-matching safety, but I've labeled it explicitly as a small, bounded sample that does not cover disk persistence, restarts, or snapshot/compaction under churn.
