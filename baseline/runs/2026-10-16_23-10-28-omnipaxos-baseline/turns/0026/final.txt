**Findings:**

1. `append_notify` can resolve with a *different* entry's decided index — and do so before its own entry has been accepted (runtime batching bookkeeping). Six deterministic repros, a randomised soak where 63–75 of 120 calls returned a wrong index, two controls; a candidate fix takes that file to 8/8 with the captured runtime suite still green.
2. Subscriptions starting below `compacted_idx` re-deliver the collapsed prefix once per tick and re-deliver decided entries, because the subscriber cursor counts items instead of log positions. Two repros; a cursor fix passes them and coexists with finding 1's fix.
3. A lagging follower is not rebuilt by the protocol itself, and a re-sent `Decide` reuses an already-consumed sequence number so it cannot take effect. Deliberately *narrowed*: source-confirmed and trace-observed, but the deterministic probe turned out to model a dead link and my leader-side fix was refuted by execution — reported as an observation with its limits, not as a defect.
4. Both bundled storages violate the documented `Storage` rule for a partially materialised interval: `PersistentStorage` returns an opaque error (executed against RocksDB), `MemoryStorage` subtracts `trimmed_idx` with a usize underflow. Core-level reachability was not demonstrated; the one shape I could not rule out is the top open item.
