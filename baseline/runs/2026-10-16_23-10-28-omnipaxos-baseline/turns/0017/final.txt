**What I found by reading `PersistentStorage`** (`omnipaxos_storage/src/persistent_storage.rs`): keys are big-endian so lexicographic order is numeric order; `append_on_prefix` sets `next_log_key = from_idx` and rewrites the tail (no subtraction, so none of the `MemoryStorage` underflow I flagged earlier); `get_entries` guards `to > next_log_key || from >= to` and otherwise seeks by key assuming contiguity; `trim` deletes the `[0, idx)` key range; `get_log_len` is `next_log_key - compacted_idx`, a subtraction that would underflow for out-of-range input. For inputs the protocol can reach (`sync_idx ≥ compacted_idx`, as analysed for the earlier suspicion), I found no off-by-one — a reading-based negative result.

**I wrote the probe and gated it, but did not execute it.** `omnipaxos_storage/tests/audit_persistent_storage_test.rs` checks exactly what the core's sync path depends on — append/read/suffix round-trip, `append_on_prefix` over a rewritten tail, `trim` + `set_compacted_idx`, `append_on_prefix` again on top of a compacted prefix, and the same invariants after reopening the directory — plus it *reports* (without asserting) what an out-of-range `append_on_prefix` does.
