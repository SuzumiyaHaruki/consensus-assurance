**Finding 3 — a lagging follower can stay behind indefinitely** (executed symptom, source-based mechanism; the report labels it that way explicitly).

A workspace-wide run surfaced a captured test failing that I hadn't touched: `sync_full_test`. Characterisation:

- Alone: 4/4 passes. With 8 CPU hogs on this 4-core box: 3/4 fail at the captured 8 s budget.
- **Slow vs stuck**: I ran the *captured test binary* against a diagnostic config copy with the budget raised to 120 s → 2 of 4 runs sat out the entire budget (122.91 s) while the passing runs finish in 2.53 s. So it is non-convergence, not slowness — a 48× margin.
- **State at the stall** (same signature in the very first observation): follower `decided = accepted = 5`, its log holding only the snapshot it merged during the sync, while the leader had `Decided(3..5), Decided(10), Decided(11), Decided(12), StopSign` — i.e. decided 9. No resend round in 120 s closed the gap. It is a liveness/availability gap, not divergence.

Mechanism I found in the source (as a hypothesis to instrument, not an observation): neither role ever re-sends `AcceptDecide`/`AcceptSync` (`resend_messages_leader` in `Phase::Accept` only re-sends Prepare/stop-sign traffic), the follower's `Phase::Accept` resend path is a no-op, an *older* sequence number is dropped silently (`MessageStatus::Outdated => ()`) so no recovery is triggered, and a re-sent `Decide` cannot help because a follower's decided index is clamped to its accepted index. Recovery therefore depends on a *newer* sequence number arriving or the user calling `reconnected(pid)` — which is why `reconnect_test` covers dropped accepts but this path does not.
