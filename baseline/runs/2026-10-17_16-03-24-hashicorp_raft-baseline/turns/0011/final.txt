The restarted node used its old `{A}` snapshot configuration while B/C used the committed `{A,B,C}` configuration. Both sides elected and committed different entries at the same index and term:

```text
index=16 A(term=3,data="real-stale",commit=16)
         BC(term=3,data="real-current",commit=16)
```

This violates the Log Matching Property and election safety. It reproduced consistently in three runs.
