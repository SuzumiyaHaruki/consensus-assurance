Continued the audit and strengthened the evidence.

The two confirmed defects are:

1. **Restart loses committed membership.** `restoreFromCommittedLogs()` advances `lastApplied` to the persisted commit index before `NewRaft()` scans configuration entries. The scan starts at `max(snapshotIndex, lastApplied)+1`, so committed configuration entries are skipped. Evidence now includes:

   - Empty configuration after restart with no snapshot.
   - Stale membership when a membership change was committed after the latest snapshot.
   - All three voters restarting empty and failing to elect a leader.
   - `GetConfiguration()` returning `{Servers:[]}` through the package-level helper.
   - A contract-conforming commit-tracking store showing the same result, ruling out an artifact of the in-memory test store.
   - Disabled-feature control passing.

2. **`NewRaft()` deadlocks on large committed logs.** Replay sends batches to `fsmMutateCh` before `runFSM` is started. With capacity 128 and `MaxAppendEntries=64`, more than 8192 entries blocks forever. A 9001-entry case hangs in the channel send; an 8000-entry control returns immediately.
