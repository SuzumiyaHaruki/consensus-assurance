Continued with the remaining feature edges. No additional consensus-safety defect was found, but three low-severity inconsistencies are now executed and recorded:

- **Commit-index staging lag:** After applying ten commands on a live leader, the in-memory `commitIndex` was `12` while the commit-tracking store still returned `11`. Commit advances driven by heartbeats or replication acknowledgements do not call `StageCommitIndex`, so `RestoreCommittedLogs` may replay fewer recently committed entries than advertised. This appears conservative rather than unsafe.
- **Commit index below snapshot:** With a snapshot at index `2` and a stale persisted commit index of `1`, a restart produced `commitIndex=1` and `lastApplied=2`. `CommitIndex()` can therefore be lower than `AppliedIndex()`, which is an API inconsistency for read-index users.
- **`LogCache` compatibility:** Wrapping a conforming commit-tracking store in `LogCache` makes `RestoreCommittedLogs` fail with `ErrIncompatibleLogStore`, because `LogCache` does not forward the optional interface.
