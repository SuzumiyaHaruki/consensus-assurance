The audit now includes a reproducible Raft safety violation, not just a restart/liveness bug.

Using the committed-configuration loss from Finding 1, I constructed two partitions:

- Partition A restored a stale `{A}` snapshot configuration while its committed log contained `{A,B,C}`.
- Partition B/C used the current `{A,B,C}` configuration.
- A elected itself alone; B and C elected with the correct two-voter quorum.
- Both sides committed commands at the same index and term:

```text
A:  term=2 data="from-stale-partition"    commit=4
BC: term=2 data="from-current-partition" commit=4
```

This violates Raft’s Log Matching Property and election safety. The result was reproduced in three consecutive runs.

I also retained the prior stale-quorum demonstration where a single stale `{A}` node elected and committed despite a committed `{A,B}` configuration, plus the lower-severity commit-index lag, commit-below-snapshot, and `LogCache` compatibility observations.
