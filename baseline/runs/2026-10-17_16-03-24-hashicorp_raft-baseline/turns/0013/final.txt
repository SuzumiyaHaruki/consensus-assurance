The audit now has both root causes reproduced end to end through normal cluster operations.

**Safety violation:** a one-voter cluster snapshotted, committed the addition of B and C, shut down, and restarted partitioned. The restarted node used its old `{A}` snapshot configuration while B/C used the committed `{A,B,C}` configuration. Both sides elected and committed different entries at the same index and term:

```text
index=16 A(term=3,data="real-stale",commit=16)
         BC(term=3,data="real-current",commit=16)
```

This violates Raft’s Log Matching Property and election safety and reproduced in three consecutive runs.

**Startup deadlock:** a real one-node cluster committed 9000 commands, shut down, and could not complete `NewRaft` after restart because restore replay filled `fsmMutateCh` before `runFSM` started.
