The split-brain consequence is now reproduced end to end through ordinary cluster operations, not only hand-built stores.

Sequence:

1. Start a one-voter cluster A and take a snapshot.
2. Add voters B and C, commit the new `{A,B,C}` configuration, and apply commands.
3. Shut down A.
4. Let B/C elect under the current configuration.
5. Restart A partitioned, with its old `{A}` snapshot.

A elected from the stale snapshot membership while B/C elected under the current membership. Both committed commands at the same index and term:

```text
index=16 A(term=3,data="real-stale",commit=16)
         BC(term=3,data="real-current",commit=16)
```

This violates Raft’s Log Matching Property and election safety. It reproduced consistently in three runs. Evidence is in `audit_results/finding1_real_cluster_conflict.txt` and `audit_results/real_cluster_conflict_run{1,2,3}.txt`.

The lower-severity observations remain:

- Commit-index persistence lags when commits advance without a new append.
- `CommitIndex()` can be below `AppliedIndex()` after a restart with a lagging snapshot.
- `LogCache` does not forward `CommitTrackingLogStore`.
