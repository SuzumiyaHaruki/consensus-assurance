Continued the audit with a check of whether commit-index persistence covers commits that advance without a new append.

New low-severity observation, now reproduced:

- A live leader reached `commitIndex=12` while its commit-tracking store still returned `11` after ten applied commands. The same path also applies when a follower advances its commit index via a heartbeat with no new entries.
- Consequence: `RestoreCommittedLogs` may replay fewer recently committed commands than advertised, although the entries remain in the log and a live leader will re-establish the commit index. This appears conservative rather than a safety violation, so it is recorded separately from the two main findings.
