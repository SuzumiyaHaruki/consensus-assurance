So I added a third concrete instance, **F3**: an `AppendEntries` carrying a `LogConfiguration` entry whose `Data` isn't valid msgpack panics `processConfigurationLogEntry` (`raft.go`, `DecodeConfiguration(entry.Data)`) — i.e., the node crashes on storing the entry, before it's even committed. The `installSnapshot/badPeers` case is the useful control: it shows the graceful error-return behavior the other three lack (and matches upstream's own `TestRaft_InstallSnapshot_InvalidPeers`).

The unifying cause: three RPC-reachable paths (`installSnapshot` Configuration, `prepareLog` default branch, `processConfigurationLogEntry`) feed unvalidated peer bytes into functions that panic, and there is **no `recover()` anywhere in the RPC main loop** — verified by grep and by the end-to-end F1 stack (`processRPC → runFollower → run`).

**Scope/severity caveat kept explicit:** all three require a peer to send a malformed message. Under the brief's crash-fault model (correct peers that only crash), a correct peer never produces this, so these are robustness/availability defects (one bad message from a buggy/Byzantine member crashes the receiver), not crash-fault-reachability safety defects. All three are inherited from upstream v1.7.3 — the tree remains byte-identical to the release, so nothing is claimed as injected.
