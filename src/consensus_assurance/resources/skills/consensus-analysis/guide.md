# Implementation understanding and bounded obligation audit

## 1. Recover the target profile
Establish the system boundary, actual contexts, execution owners and storage/transport/application interfaces from code. selection_injection describes constructor/factory/caller selection and adapter ownership; variants describes actual protocol/configuration paths. Fault assumptions belong to the selected audit question. Caller injection does not externalize built-in repository implementations: inspect the chosen boundary and identify available variants without assuming activation.

## 2. Describe incomplete but coherent implementation behavior
Use seven parallel Activity coordinates from activity-classes.md, not seven execution stages. Each has sourced applicability/boundary or specific unknowns. Reverse-check APIs, handlers, timers, storage callbacks, recovery and deletion paths using one surfaces list. Retain UNCLASSIFIED_PROTOCOL_RESPONSIBILITY when necessary. Do not generate correctness claims in descriptive discovery.

A Behavior is one actor/execution owner handling one trigger in a concrete context. Split independent actors or branches establishing different semantic results before declaring fact edges. Keep legal prehistory, guards, reads/writes, durable/external effects, asynchronous boundaries, protections and unknowns.

A Fact asserts: given identity/context X, semantic assertion Y has been established. Observing a signal, requesting a transition and completing that transition are distinct assertions. Unknown producers or consumers are legal when recorded in unknowns. Behavior.produces_fact_ids/consumes_fact_ids are authoritative; reverse indices and cross-Activity relationships are derived. Do not bypass an unread semantic establishment merely because the adjacent endpoints are known.

## 3. Interleave bounded breadth and depth
AuditSpec is progressive, not an enumeration prerequisite for testing. Start with an incomplete skeleton. Select a sourced candidate or a specific deferred Surface read according to the next discriminator; an unmapped Surface needs no invented Behavior or Fact. Ready evidence work and active candidates take priority. Explained or evidence-blocked candidates may yield to queued descriptive feedback or a selected frontier; do not chain candidate selection inside depth work. A mapped Surface is not complete coverage.

Use focused spec_refine deltas to add/split/reassign behaviors, refine/split facts, remove unsupported edges, and correct applicability, surfaces or ownership. Required diagnostic source belongs in the first refinement packet. Preserve unaccepted drafts and explicit unknowns; correcting an inventory is not evidence of correctness. Representation typos use mechanical repair; decomposition does not. An accepted normative unit's referenced fact meaning, identity or validity context requires F2 and explicit reconnection.

## 4. Select a question, then review source before deriving an obligation
Select one accepted principal Fact lifecycle: establishment, preservation, consumption or recovery. Persist a structured AuditQuestion with a decisive discriminator, actual scenario, relevant behaviors, protections, unknowns and consequence in importance. A selected question can use depth before any obligation or unit exists. Read only the causal dependencies needed to resolve its discriminator; keep acquired and deferred receipt items explicit.

Finding an existing protection and closing a suspicion is a useful audit outcome, not failed derivation or correctness evidence. Return explained_by_existing_mechanism with sources, scope and limitations and no obligation. Source review has four outcomes: continue exact reading, explain, retain evidence-blocked, or derive an obligation.

Factor a complex question into: (1) a local semantic relation at a producer/consumer, interface, state or recovery boundary; (2) reachability in a legal history; and (3) a broader consequence. A local obligation may precede the other two when actual source identifies the boundary, an interface/API/state responsibility supplies the normative expectation, an independent oracle can compare the relation, and the result discriminates the parent. Neither tests nor models require advance proof of failure. A local violation remains local and does not establish its parent consequence.

Before retaining evidence-blocked, inspect the meaningful local relations already identified. If only reachability or broader consequence is missing, an ID-linked child may preserve the parent while checking a separately grounded local relation; do not require every blocked parent to fork. The child records its own normative basis, necessary prehistory, stopping observation and excluded consequences. A completed child observation leaves the Parent's broader unknowns unchanged. Do not fork mere asymmetry, weak representation or suspicious code without a sourced expectation and independent discriminator, and do not describe an observable difference as a contract violation until applicability is established. needs_specific_evidence means no attributable check is supported for the parent or its identified local relations, not merely that the final consequence is unproved. Missing selected application/deployment behavior can justify that result; unread source of a pinned build dependency does not block a target-interface-grounded local obligation and can be observed later through the actual build input.

Otherwise narrow the same question through source review, or derive one primary obligation with normative/contract basis separate from observed guards. A second hypothesis may concern the same Fact/lifecycle when its object, context or event relation changes. Explicitly pause and link an active parent when one independent question can be checked first, retaining applicable counterevidence and unknowns. Supporting obligations are dependencies, not additional simultaneously checked requirements. Source-grounded reusable implementation omissions or mismatches go to spec_refine. Only requires_recheck corrections that change the current discriminator or judgment reconnect the same Fact/lifecycle. independent_enrichment may concern selected objects but never blocks a sourced terminal decision; retain pending feedback when resources end. Candidate-only protection arguments stay on AuditQuestion; ordinary reading is not inventory correction. Avoid combining unrelated responsibility layers into one unit.

## 5. Obtain evidence and continue
Use source_review for uncertain semantics/applicability, direct_test for a controllable execution with an independent oracle, controlled_schedule for a few critical orderings with actual schedule control, and local_model for complex legal histories. Code defines behavior; the obligation defines the checker. Finite pass or TLC holds is not implementation proof; a local violation does not automatically establish a broader consequence.

Prefer ready high-value checks, then decisive selected-source gaps, then high-consequence unmapped surfaces. Ordinary unknowns remain inventory. After evidence, record the scoped conclusion and limitations, update understanding and choose the next obligation. After disposed candidates, if no new tractable discriminator is selected, stop selection provisionally with visible gaps. Accepted understanding or apparent coverage is not audit success. No fixed protocol properties, target-specific answers or historical bugs are required.

Lifecycle meanings: establishment checks whether the fact a behavior claims to establish actually holds; preservation checks that an established fact remains valid until consumption; consumption checks whether a representation is interpreted as a stronger guarantee than it establishes; recovery checks re-establishment after crash, restart, loss or explicit invalidation. Continuing after an error alone is not recovery; acknowledgement from stale metadata usually raises establishment or consumption questions.

Externalized means the current audit boundary excludes the concrete implementation responsibility, not merely constructor injection. For caller-selected interfaces with in-repository variants, distinguish external selection, available implementations and an unknown currently used variant. Do not externalize unread built-in storage failure semantics by assumption.

Compare semantically related paths using the cross-path differential method in behavior-facts.md. Start with the weakest sourced Fact and inspect its actual consumer before deriving a stronger requirement.
