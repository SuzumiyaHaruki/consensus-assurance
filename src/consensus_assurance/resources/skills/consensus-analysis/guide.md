# Source-grounded consensus investigation

Candidate records a sourced question, protections and unknowns. Obligation states an applicable responsibility; Check supplies a direct test or controlled schedule; Evidence records attributed observations. Source explanation and bounded negative results are useful outcomes; an obligation is not confirmation.

Use the authoritative activity_roles in research.json: two core relationships, supported by five implementation mechanisms. A question may concern either core relationship. Never infer relevance from a filename, protocol label or an Activity tag. Establish actual ownership, context, support and consumption from source; protocols need not have a leader, log, global term, self-vote or fixed majority.

First build a sourced overall explanation of both core lines and their connection in AuditSpec.core_overview. Trace a representative operation from entry through actual owners, support production, identity and eligibility, aggregation and decision conditions to recording, propagation, application or public completion. Trace how this implementation acquires, changes and loses its context or authority, including how old requests, replies, pending support and established decisions survive, exit or are reconfirmed. Follow real callees, producers and consumers in both directions; compare important reject, stale, retry and asynchronous paths. General CFT knowledge supplies questions, not attributed requirements or invented target behavior, including when protocol=none.

Read the membership, history, recovery or completion mechanisms needed to explain these paths now; deeper backend variants can remain open. A Behavior describes an actual owner, trigger, guards, effects and alternatives. A Fact states the weakest assertion the implementation establishes for an identified object and context. Produces/consumes edges are authoritative; reverse indexes are derived. Save partial maps and early anomalies as sourced Surfaces without claiming readiness for focused investigation. Distinguish open_details from core_gaps: an unread failure variant may be a detail; unexplained decision formation, authority transition or old-work handling breaks the initial backbone. Mark unavailable source or permission boundaries explicitly. Usable is a sourced judgment, not proof or a demand to read every file. Keep the overview about mechanisms and important connections; local details belong in Behavior/Fact/Surface. Execution progress belongs to CheckRun and its current assessment, not overview unknowns.

Only a user-configured directed_question permits a proportionate local boundary before this overall explanation. activity_focus alone does not waive either initial core line. Otherwise use next_objective in research.json: recover the missing backbone, then choose a local discriminator. A usable overview and the first Candidate may be submitted together. Local exploration requires neither a fabricated Fact nor an obligation.

Select one principal Fact lifecycle and a concrete A1/A2 relationship. question.behavior_ids names direct establish/consume/invalidate/reinterpret roles. supporting_behavior_ids separately names sourced prehistory, context or consequence support and why it matters. An A6 implementation can support A1 through a concrete cross_activity_effects explanation; do not relabel it. Keep normative statements, code observations, test assertions, assumptions and hypotheses separate. Source identity does not prove meaning.

Use existing question, Behavior owner/context and Harness legality to identify the selected entry, serialization owner, storage/application/network duties and the observation that means completion. Shared internals do not imply identical API permissions; neither high nor low level is inherently preferable. If a needed permission belongs to another entry, compare explaining the correspondence, using that entry directly, and cheap exploration before fixing a heavy check. Choose the path that answers the limiting premise with obtainable evidence.

Separate conditional behavior, contract applicability and correctness. Missing responsibility limits attribution, not the value of every observation: when a concrete caller policy distinguishes explanations, use existing explore question/rationale/feedback to retain the actual producer prefix, policy, inputs, effects, substitutions and remaining responsibility. A source explanation may suffice; avoid repeating arbitrary policies without information gain. Calls do not establish callee effects; independent executions do not form one history. Track whether protections run, are finitely delayed or omitted. Actual producers can justify a prefix without process-birth replay; assigned critical state remains conditional. Compare this useful observation, a sourced explanation or another relationship before treating a local contract gap as a run-wide obstacle.

Shared Behavior/Fact relationships and parent links do not propagate conclusions. Ground obligations in applicable expectations, not code differences. Express relations over objects and contexts; concrete identities and timings belong to Check, while applicability-defining modes, counts and faults belong to Claim/Scope. A small test does not weaken the requirement; abstraction does not prove global safety. Source explanation needs no fabricated execution, and a local violation need not prove its suspected cause or wider consequence.

Keep the Candidate and Unit for unchanged requirements and scope. Follow the repair rules in skills/evidence-review/guide.md; wider consequences may form independent questions.

At explanation, bounded completion, confirmation, pause or repair completion, save the sourced answer and next discriminator in feedback. Add stable producer/consumer relationships to AuditSpec before later questions depend on them; keep input-specific observations in execution/feedback. Reuse adequate maps; update core_overview for important connections. Independent additions preserve old results; counterevidence challenges its specific interpretation. Use research.json explorations and handoff locators to read fixed questions, inputs and completed tool results, including after interruption; continuing the same run does not require rediscovery or reexecution. Cite the actual execution in feedback without promoting conditional observations to property conclusions. Historical remaining is not current execution status; update semantic unknowns through question_updates, while CheckRun owns execution progress.

Use Codex search, editing and permitted feedback within the snapshot/draft boundary. Choose direct experiments or controlled schedules for the actual discriminator. Prefer finishing a concrete accepted check; justify a useful switch with pause/feedback. Near the deadline submit small complete sourced feedback for new knowledge; no extra closing report is needed to repeat saved results. The controller reports natural expiry from retained state. Never manufacture closure with an incomplete review.

Evidence of one complete violation and completion of all required scenarios are different quantifiers. Independent partial operations remain visible without erasing a valid witness; ambiguity affecting its own identity or history still blocks it. A passing process, provisional review, absent issues or locally checked Unit is not overall correctness. The controller records pending work at the authorized boundary even if the last explanation is incomplete.

Consensus safety has priority, with liveness under explicit environment/fault/scheduling or deadline assumptions. A sourced related implementation-contract violation can be complete in its own scope without inventing a stronger consensus consequence. General code issues stay secondary. Finite tool timeout alone proves neither holds nor general liveness failure.

After each local disposition use the overview and relationship navigation to choose a useful next step: a remaining premise, another actual consumer/context of the same Fact, or an independent sourced discriminator. This is not a required three-item form or subsystem rotation. Local obstacles do not block every relationship; mapped does not mean settled. Record the chosen unknown, protection and expected information gain in existing trigger_rationale/feedback, without another selection product. Reading, explanation, exploration, repair, review or knowledge retention can be worthwhile without promising a complete new finding. Reuse valid maps and checks; new work within authorization needs no further user instruction.

pending_work is selected execution/review debt, not the whole research frontier. In an open run, continue useful source investigation, explanation, exploration, checking or knowledge retention until the controller reaches the authorized time/call boundary. A missing complete next check, settled registered questions or paused local contracts do not end the run. When execution, Unit, review or revision capacity is exhausted, choose useful work supported by the remaining capabilities; never fabricate review or sleep to consume time. Near the deadline, choose a smaller informative step or sourced handoff. Do not reset budgets, repeat empty products or expand authorization. Only a user-preconfigured finite directed question can complete early after its scoped task is disposed. Actual cancellation, safety refusal, permission loss and unrecoverable service/tool failures still stop immediately; the controller records those facts.
