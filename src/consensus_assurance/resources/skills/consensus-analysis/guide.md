# Source-grounded consensus investigation

Candidate is a sourced question worth investigating: its requirements, protections or legal history may still be unknown. Obligation fixes an applicable responsibility without presuming its result. Check supplies concrete inputs, history, controls and observations; Evidence/Review retain measurements and assess correspondence. Missing normative authority calls for contract investigation, missing history for source work or conditional exploration. Neither gap bars a Candidate or justifies an invented obligation.

Use activity_roles in research.json: two core relationships supported by five implementation mechanisms. A question may concern either core line. Protocol names, files and Activity labels do not establish relevance; actual ownership, context, support and consumption do. General CFT knowledge supplies questions, not target requirements, including when protocol=none.

First explain both core lines and their connection in AuditSpec.core_overview. Follow a representative operation through its owners, support production, identity/eligibility, aggregation and decision conditions to recording, propagation, application or public completion. Explain how context or authority is acquired, changed and lost, and how old requests, replies, pending support and established decisions survive, exit or require reconfirmation. Trace callers and callees in both directions, including important reject, stale, retry and asynchronous paths. Read the membership, history, recovery and completion mechanisms needed for this backbone; leave deeper variants as specific unknowns.

Save partial maps and early leads as sourced Surfaces. Distinguish open_details from core_gaps: an unread variant can remain open; unexplained decision formation, authority transition or old-work handling breaks readiness. Usable is a sourced judgment, not proof or exhaustive reading. Only a user-configured directed_question permits a proportionate local boundary first; activity_focus does not. Follow next_objective; a usable overview and first Candidate may share a product. Local exploration needs neither a fabricated Fact nor an obligation.

Use behavior-facts.md for the shared implementation account. Select a principal Fact lifecycle with a concrete A1/A2 relationship. Direct behavior_ids establish, consume, invalidate or reinterpret it; supporting_behavior_ids explain distinct history, context or consequence support. A support Activity can contribute through a concrete cross_activity_effects relationship without relabeling. Keep requirements, observed code, test assertions, assumptions and hypotheses separate. Citation identity does not prove meaning.

Choose an important, tractable discriminator over merely constructing a complicated check. Compare whether more work on the current premise changes judgment, an existing guard explains it, or another sourced relationship offers a clearer distinction. Use existing trigger_rationale/feedback, without rankings, quotas, mandatory alternatives or subsystem rotation. A guard settles only its demonstrated branch. Mapped, referenced or one local holds does not settle a Behavior or Fact. Reuse existing source understanding, legal-prefix construction and control code for independent questions; each formal claim still needs its own fixed inputs, actual execution and correspondence.

Identify the actual entry, serialization owner, storage/application/network duties and completion meaning through the question, Behavior and Harness legality. Shared internals do not imply identical API permissions. When permission belongs to another entry, compare source explanation, that entry and a small exploration before a heavy test. Conditional exploration can retain caller policy, producer prefix, inputs, effects and substitutions while contract applicability remains unresolved. Do not repeat arbitrary policies without information gain. Actual producers may establish a finite prefix without process-birth replay; assigning critical state remains conditional. A call edge or separate executions do not establish one legal history.

Consensus safety has priority; liveness needs explicit environment, fault, schedule or deadline assumptions. Local validity, eligibility, wakeup or correlation duties can be fully answered with applicable requirements and independent observations. They do not inherit every prerequisite of client-visible safety or general liveness. Conversely, an internal difference cannot establish a client consequence. Preserve wider consequences as limits or independent questions. Neither shared Facts nor parent links propagate conclusions. Keep the Candidate/Unit when the requirement and scope are unchanged; use the evidence-review repair rules for actual changes.

At natural handoffs, retain the new answer and next discriminator. Save stable relationships before another question depends on them; do not rewrite the map after every read. Use CheckSubmission.candidate for a map plus obligation/check, or the next substantive research product for knowledge updates. ReviewSubmission carries feedback, not a map. Use separate research immediately only when shared misunderstanding needs correction. Cite prior arguments rather than repeating them: Claim/Scope owns the duty, Harness this history, Monitor the measurement, Review correspondence, Feedback new learning.

Use exploration and handoff locators for retained questions, inputs and observations; same-run continuity needs no rediscovery or reexecution. Attribute the actual execution in feedback. Independent additions can preserve old artifact bases; concrete counterevidence challenges the specific interpretation through existing map_changes and preserves/challenges. No-op maps create no version. Keep current semantic unknowns in question_updates, execution progress in CheckRun/assessment and historical answers in their original records.

Open runs continue useful investigation until controller time/call or actual interruption boundaries. A missing complete next check, empty pending_work or disposed registered questions does not end the run; reselect from the overview, shared relationships, Surfaces and unread paths. Exhausted execution, Unit, review or revision capacity limits those actions, while remaining source work is still useful. Near expiry choose a smaller informative step or sourced handoff, not a closing essay. Never reset budgets, fabricate closure, repeat empty products or sleep to consume time. Only a preconfigured finite directed task may complete early after disposition. Actual cancellation, safety refusal, permission loss and unrecoverable service/tool failures still stop immediately.

A complete violating witness and completion of all independent scenarios are different quantifiers. Preserve valid local results and independent gaps; ambiguity affecting the witness still blocks attribution. Process success, provisional review or checked Units never prove overall correctness. Tool timeout alone establishes neither holds nor general liveness failure.
