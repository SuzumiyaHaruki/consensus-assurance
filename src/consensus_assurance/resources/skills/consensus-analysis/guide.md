# Source-grounded consensus investigation

Candidate is a sourced question worth investigating, with protections, unknowns and direction history. Obligation is an applicable correctness responsibility whose satisfaction remains to be checked. Check specifies a concrete test, controlled schedule or model of that requirement. Evidence records attributed execution or observations, not confidence. Explanation or a bounded negative result is useful research; forming an obligation is not confirmation.

Use the authoritative activity_roles in research.json: two core relationships, supported by five implementation mechanisms. A question may concern either core relationship. Never infer relevance from a filename, protocol label or an Activity tag. Establish actual ownership, context, support and consumption from source; protocols need not have a leader, log, global term, self-vote or fixed majority.

First build a sourced overall explanation of both core lines and their connection in AuditSpec.core_overview. Trace a representative operation from entry through actual owners, support production, identity and eligibility, aggregation and decision conditions to recording, propagation, application or public completion. Trace how this implementation acquires, changes and loses its context or authority, including how old requests, replies, pending support and established decisions survive, exit or are reconfirmed. Follow real callees, producers and consumers in both directions; compare important reject, stale, retry and asynchronous paths. General CFT knowledge supplies questions, not attributed requirements or invented target behavior, including when protocol=none.

Read the membership, history, recovery or completion mechanisms needed to explain these paths now; deeper backend variants can remain open. A Behavior describes an actual owner, trigger, guards, effects and alternatives. A Fact states the weakest assertion the implementation establishes for an identified object and context. Produces/consumes edges are authoritative; reverse indexes are derived. Save partial maps and early anomalies as sourced Surfaces without claiming readiness for focused investigation. Distinguish open_details from core_gaps: an unread failure variant may be a detail; unexplained decision formation, authority transition or old-work handling breaks the initial backbone. Mark unavailable source or permission boundaries explicitly. Usable is a sourced judgment, not proof or a demand to read every file. Keep the overview about mechanisms and important connections; local details belong in Behavior/Fact/Surface. Execution progress belongs to CheckRun and its current assessment, not overview unknowns.

Only a user-configured directed_question permits a proportionate local boundary before this overall explanation. activity_focus alone does not waive either initial core line. Otherwise use next_objective in research.json: recover the missing backbone, then choose a local discriminator. A usable overview and the first Candidate may be submitted together. Local exploration requires neither a fabricated Fact nor an obligation.

Select one principal Fact lifecycle and a concrete A1/A2 relationship. question.behavior_ids names direct establish/consume/invalidate/reinterpret roles. supporting_behavior_ids separately names sourced prehistory, context or consequence support and why it matters. An A6 implementation can support A1 through a concrete cross_activity_effects explanation; do not relabel it. Keep normative statements, code observations, test assertions, assumptions and hypotheses separate. Source identity does not prove meaning.

Use existing question, Behavior owner/context and Harness legality to identify the selected entry, serialization owner, storage/application/network duties and the observation that means completion. Shared internals do not imply identical API permissions; neither high nor low level is inherently preferable. If a needed permission belongs to another entry, compare explaining the correspondence, using that entry directly, and cheap exploration before fixing a heavy check. Choose the path that answers the limiting premise with obtainable evidence.

Separate requirement, producer, prefix, legal same-execution history and observed endpoint gaps. Calls do not establish callee effects; independent executions do not form one history. Explain whether protections run, are finitely delayed or omitted with sourced noninterference reasoning. A constructed prefix may be justified by actual producers without starting at process birth; direct assignment of critical state remains conditional. Actively investigate the tractable gap that most changes the current judgment.

Shared Behavior/Fact relationships belong to no Candidate; parent links do not propagate conclusions. Derive an obligation from an applicable source expectation, not the observed code difference. Express the required relation over relevant objects and contexts; instance names, operation IDs and waiting times normally belong to the Check. Keep counts, deployment modes or fault assumptions in Claim/Scope when they actually determine applicability. A small test does not weaken the requirement, and a parameterized requirement is not a global proof. Source explanation can settle the stated suspicion without execution Evidence; a local interface violation need not establish its suspected cause or a wider application failure.

Keep the Candidate and Unit when the requirement and scope remain unchanged. A driver repair answers its exact old premise with new sourced legality; preserve fixed history and unanswered counterevidence without copying obsolete driver conditions into the new plan. Changed normative applicability, fault/configuration scope or claim meaning uses F2; F3 expands and reconnects dependencies while preserving the proposition. Changed executed observation/oracle encoding needs explicit correction, fresh execution and review. Wider consequences or cause isolation may become separate questions without reopening a completed local result.

At explanation, bounded completion, confirmation, pause or repair completion, save the new sourced answer and next discriminator in feedback. A source protection may settle a suspicion without a test; execute when actual production, scheduling or completion can change the judgment. Add stable producer/consumer relationships to AuditSpec before later questions depend on them; keep input-specific observations in execution/feedback. Reuse adequate maps and update core_overview only for important connections. Independent additions preserve old results; actual counterevidence challenges its specific interpretation. Read earlier handoffs through their locators; remaining is historical. Update current semantic unknowns through question_updates; CheckRun owns execution progress.

Use Codex search, editing and permitted feedback within the snapshot/draft boundary. Choose direct experiments or optional models for the actual discriminator. Prefer finishing a concrete accepted check; justify a useful switch with pause/feedback. Near the deadline inspect completed execution, pending correspondence and exact issues, then save a small complete answer if feasible. Do not restart broad inventory or submit an incomplete review to manufacture closure.

Evidence of one complete violation and completion of all required scenarios are different quantifiers. Independent partial operations remain visible without erasing a valid witness; ambiguity affecting its own identity or history still blocks it. A passing process, model holds, provisional review, absent issues or locally checked Unit is not overall correctness. Stop honestly within authorization and budgets; the controller records pending work even if the last explanation is incomplete.

Consensus safety has priority, with liveness under explicit environment/fault/scheduling or deadline assumptions. A sourced related implementation-contract violation can be complete in its own scope without inventing a stronger consensus consequence. General code issues stay secondary. Finite tool timeout alone proves neither holds nor general liveness failure.

After each local disposition return to the updated overview and frontier: an unresolved valuable discriminator, a new independent question, mapped relationships not covered by this result, or unexpanded core/support surfaces. Compare real next steps by consequence, protections, unknowns, feasibility and how their outcomes change understanding; group related references rather than enumerate every unknown. New questions within the authorized run need no new user instruction. Insufficient evidence for a broader consequence or a conflicting local contract limits that question, not the run. An empty pending_work list means selected work is disposed, not that investigation is exhausted. Shared Facts, contexts and parent links do not make different questions identical. Paused unaccepted drafts remain leads, not accepted Candidates or Evidence. Do not impose activity rotation or discovery quotas.

For an early run stop, explain why the best remaining source investigation, local exploration, check or review cannot usefully advance with current time, calls and permissions. Mark a step actionable whenever it can still obtain useful evidence, even without a new obligation or a complete check. Finished Units, an unresolved parent, a need to start another discriminator, and refusal to spend budget for its own sake are not reasons to mark such a step unactionable. Preserve unresolved scope and concrete resumption conditions when no useful feasible step remains; this permits stopping before all unknowns or budgets are exhausted. A rejected stop requires reconsidering the decision, not relabeling the same rationale as insufficient_basis or no_actionable_direction. Local pause is a handoff; global resource, permission, authentication, infrastructure or user interruptions remain immediate exits. New autonomous runs never import prior research; directed capability cases are opt-in, not default answers.
