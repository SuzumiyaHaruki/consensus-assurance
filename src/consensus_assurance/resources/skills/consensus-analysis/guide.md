# Investigation and selection

Candidate investigates a sourced question, including unknown requirements, protection premises or legal history. Obligation fixes an applicable responsibility without presuming violation; Check supplies inputs, history and observations. Missing authority calls for contract investigation, missing history for source work or conditional exploration. Neither gap bars a Candidate or licenses an invented obligation.

First explain both core relationships from activity_roles and their connection in AuditSpec.core_overview. Follow representative work through owners, qualified support, aggregation, decision, propagation and consumption; explain acquisition, change and loss of context/authority and handling of old requests, replies, pending support and established decisions. Read the supporting membership, history, recovery and completion mechanisms needed for this backbone. Trace callers and callees, important rejection, retry and asynchronous paths; deeper variants may remain open.

Save early discrepancies as sourced Surfaces or specific unknowns even when no obligation is ready. Distinguish open_details from core_gaps: unexplained formation, authority transition or old-work handling breaks readiness; a usable overview can retain local anomalies. Only a preconfigured directed_question permits a proportionate local boundary first; activity_focus does not. A usable map and first Candidate may share a product. Unmapped source work and exploration need no fabricated Fact.

Use behavior-facts.md for implementation meaning. Select a principal Fact lifecycle with a sourced A1/A2 connection; a question need not span both. Direct behavior_ids establish, consume, invalidate or reinterpret it; supporting_behavior_ids explain separate history/context/consequence support. Cross-activity effects establish actual relationships without relabeling support Activities. Names and protocol labels establish neither relevance nor responsibility.

Use question, counterevidence, unknowns, importance and trigger_rationale to identify what remains unexplained, what existing protection does establish, and what the next action will distinguish. A concise sourced difference suffices; no ranking, mandatory alternative list or confidence threshold. Compare contract reading, smaller exploration and another known relationship before committing to a costly test. “This input has not been run” alone is not a discriminator. An empty unknowns list does not disqualify a check whose earlier investigation established its value.

When the actual mechanism explains the suspicion and no important execution premise remains, use explained or sourced feedback and reselect without manufacturing a Unit. An explicit guard with unresolved ownership, timing, producer or context instead calls for investigation of that premise. A negative result should identify the plausible explanation it excluded and independent premises left open; distinguish it from execution corroborating an already-explained protection. Neither settles the entire Fact or becomes a reason to expand input variants indefinitely. Keep one requirement's independent scenarios together; a genuinely different responsibility gets its own question.

Conditional differences with unclear contracts remain exploration or local pause. Once responsibility is grounded, obligation and construction may be separate products. Reuse helpers and legal prefixes only after checking their entry, caller duties, completion order and fault assumptions for this question. A convenient driver must not exclude permitted overlap that supplied the discriminator, nor assume a looser ordering is legal because it produces a difference. Fixed checks must still exercise the investigated premise; a narrower child cannot settle its parent. Use existing F2/F3 when requirement or scope changes. Finish necessary technical repair and review of accepted work; switching questions preserves its debt.

Prioritize consensus safety and explicitly bounded liveness. Independently grounded validity, eligibility, accounting, wakeup or correlation duties can be resolved locally without a client endpoint or global consequence. Potential severity does not let a convenient assertion substitute for the intended question. Shared facts, helpers and parent links transfer understanding, not results.

At handoffs identify the answered proposition, supporting sources/execution and independent remaining question in feedback. At the next substantive product already updating the relevant Behavior, Fact or overview, or preparing a Candidate that relies on that knowledge, replace the answered original sentence with its sourced conditional relationship and remove obsolete execution/review reminders. Preserve independent unknowns and historical inputs. Consult the actual answer and assessment; an understanding label or exact reference does not establish resolution. Correct misleading explanations before reuse, without a dedicated cleanup turn or unrelated whole-map sweep. Source reading and exploration may continue from feedback; near expiry save minimal complete learning. Use behavior-facts.md for field ownership and tasks/audit.md for product references.

Open runs continue investigation until controller time/call or actual interruption boundaries. Empty pending_work or disposed registered questions does not exhaust the frontier: use the overview, other consumers/contexts, Surfaces and unread paths. Execution, Unit, review or revision limits restrict those actions, not remaining authorized source work. Near expiry take a smaller informative step; do not reset budgets, repeat empty work or sleep to fill time. Only a preconfigured finite directed task may complete early. Actual cancellation, safety refusal, permission loss and unrecoverable service/tool failure still stop.
