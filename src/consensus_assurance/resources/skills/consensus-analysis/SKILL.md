---
name: consensus-analysis
description: Select sourced CFT questions, trace causal responsibilities and correct understanding from evidence.
---

Use consensus formation/progress and context/authority change as complementary guides. Read representative producer, aggregation, decision, handoff and consumption paths, including what happens to old work across a transition. Follow membership, recovery, history, application and client completion where they matter. No global leader, log, term or majority is assumed. Develop this broad picture alongside local questions; neither a two-line overview nor Activity/Behavior/Fact labels admit or reject an investigation.

Ask a concrete sourced uncertainty, including missing requirements or legal history. Trace who produces the relevant state/message, what the consumer requires, and which admission, serialization, dispatch, reset or other consumer might supply an apparently absent local guard. Distinguish normative contracts, actual code, test expectations and hypotheses. Select the next fact that would change the answer; an untested variant alone is not a useful discriminator. An adequate source explanation can dispose of a suspicion without execution. Another question may proceed while this one remains open; selecting it does not pause or resolve its neighbors.

When useful, save partial shared understanding: Behavior describes actual conditional effects; Fact describes what those effects establish for an identity/context; Obligation states what an applicable sourced contract requires. Preserve different guards/error paths, persisted versus published state, notification versus completed work, object identity, membership qualification and context changes. Shared writes do not imply shared callbacks or follow-up effects. Map references are navigation and explicit dependencies, not a universal repository decomposition or a completeness certificate.

Prefer actual constructors, proposals, message producers, storage and handlers with controlled environmental delivery, delay, permitted storage failure, timers, disconnection or restart. Check target test utilities against the premise being investigated. Reuse a real initialization-to-trigger prefix instead of fabricating the decisive vote, promise, commitment or completion state. Deterministic handler delivery and in-memory transports are valid when they preserve actual caller/scheduling contracts; a transport wrapper cannot call arbitrary reordering a delay on a guaranteed FIFO path. Do not alter protocol logic.

Constructed states and internal interface probes are useful conditional exploration. Record which premises the test supplies. For a claim depending on normal reachability, pursue the actual producer and prefix; a sourced local interface contract may itself permit an input without a distributed deployment. An initialization-equivalent fixture needs concrete correspondence for relevant fields; otherwise run the prefix. A real snapshot can shorten history only for state it retains, not missing workers, in-flight messages, request identities or membership context. Do not join independent histories merely because numeric indices match.

After an anomaly, investigate its decisive missing causal link: the real producer/prefix, enclosing protection, same-operation endpoint, actual applied/read state, or owner of continuing work. Distinguish implementation-owned continuation from later caller duties; finite waiting alone proves neither a deadline breach nor permanent stalling. Preserve independently established local defects without demanding their worst conceivable cluster consequence. Retain an unproductive gap and reselect instead of pursuing it indefinitely.

At a natural handoff save the sourced answer, actual execution references and current remaining questions. Correct the relevant premise before reusing it, retaining its old version and the specifically affected interpretation. Independent additions need no impact essay about unrelated objects. Shared names alone do not invalidate results. No map-cleanup, translation, extra review-only turn or finding quota is required. A child's result does not settle a different parent claim.
