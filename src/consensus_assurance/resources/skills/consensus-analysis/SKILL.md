---
name: consensus-analysis
description: Investigate sourced CFT questions and their missing causal links.
---

Use consensus formation/progress and context/authority change as complementary guides. Follow representative producers, decisions, handoffs and consumers, including old work across transitions and relevant membership, recovery, persistence and completion duties. No universal leader, log, term or majority is assumed. Broad orientation and local investigation refine each other; maps and Activity/Behavior/Fact labels are optional.

Ask a concrete sourced uncertainty, including missing requirements or legal history. Trace producers, consumer duties and enclosing admission, serialization, dispatch or reset protections. Separate normative contracts, actual code, test expectations and hypotheses. Choose a discriminator that could change the answer; an untested variant alone is not a reason to run it. Sourced explanations and negative results need no compulsory test. Switching questions does not pause or resolve others.

Optional maps describe conditional effects (Behavior), what they establish for an identity/context (Fact), and applicable responsibilities (Obligation). Preserve error paths, persisted versus published state, notification versus completion and membership/context changes. Shared writes imply neither shared callbacks nor conclusions. References provide navigation and explicit dependencies, not completeness.

For a protocol-reachability claim, prefer permitted initialization through actual producers to the violating endpoint. First inspect constructors, test utilities and normal API/message paths. Small in-process instances, deterministic delivery, logical ticks and in-memory storage are appropriate when contracts hold. Control delivery, delay, disconnection, crash/restart or permitted storage failure at real boundaries; respect FIFO guarantees. Hold an actual reply without altering it. Generate decisive log, ballot, membership or completion state through prior calls. Rewriting a successful durable value is not a crash.

Direct handler calls and state injection remain useful conditional probes: identify what they bypass. A constructor accepting storage, an absent private guard or a TODO does not permit arbitrary histories. Checkpoints need recorded production history and relevant retained state; disk bytes alone lose workers, pending futures and in-flight replies. Source-justified prefix reduction must preserve relevant state, identities and scheduling opportunities: say what was omitted and why, or run the prefix when simpler. Separate producer/consumer fragments with matching numeric terms or indices do not establish one compatible history.

After a conditional anomaly, reduce its decisive missing link: producer, initial state, context transition, permitted schedule/fault or actual endpoint. Prefer extending the harness so real code creates the condition; another consumer of invented state adds no reachability. Alternatively substantiate an enclosing protection or independent local contract, or pause with the unresolved discriminator. No attempt quota or compulsory deployment applies.

For applied effects observe this operation's real application/result; entry presence and counters are insufficient. For progress identify the applicable continuation duty and permitted schedule; finite waiting is not an infinite-stall proof. Preserve valid local results without promoting them to a parent protocol consequence. At a useful handoff retain the observed answer, supplied premise and source read or executable prefix that could settle it. Update attributed knowledge while retaining previous interpretations; do not repeat the map, claim or earlier evidence.
