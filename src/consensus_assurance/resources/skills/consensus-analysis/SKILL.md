# Consensus investigation

Use consensus formation/progress and context/authority transitions as complementary lines: how decisions and completions become justified, and how that justification survives authority acquisition, transfer or loss. Membership, persistence, recovery, asynchronous work and client contracts connect them. Other Activities provide implementation support, not mandatory labels or stages. Broad orientation and local traces refine one another; a sourced local question or probe needs no global map, principal Fact or settled requirement.

A Behavior describes actual conditional execution: guards, ordering, state changes, messages, returns and completion. Use the coarsest granularity preserving materially different failure, visibility, ownership, persistence and asynchronous paths. A Fact expresses what those behaviors establish for an identified object and context under explicit conditions. Required behavior, code-established assertions and one execution's observations differ. A source assertion may precede testing; an observed instance is not a universal guarantee. Neither vacuous True nor an expected property copied from documentation is a useful Fact.

Trace what producers establish, transmit and retain and what consumers rely on, including intervening admission, serialization, reset and completion duties. Asymmetry alone is not a defect; guards, thresholds, names and existing tests do not prove freshness, eligibility or same-operation association. A short hypothesis and discriminator should identify the applicable responsibility, apparent mismatch and observation or enclosing protection that would refute it. Source-explained negatives need equal care. Missing normative support can be investigated; current behavior or a TODO does not create a universal contract. Do not disclose private chain of thought or fill an exhaustive checklist.

Global A1/A2 orientation ↔ local producer/consumer inquiry → evidence and knowledge correction → global re-orientation is a research cycle, not required products or turns. Reading, construction and repair may share a turn; source reasoning can settle a suspicion without testing. For bounded liveness, connect a legal trigger and repeated nonprogress to a stable source-supported blocking mechanism and useful controls; apply evidence-review without demanding an infinite run.

Promote Behavior/Fact knowledge when reused or needing durable correction, with sources, observations and conditions. Uses identify entry revision and the assertion relied upon. If new evidence changes a relied-on scope or cause, inspect that saved assertion and revise its existing id; a new Fact cannot substitute for correcting an inaccurate Behavior/Fact. Distinguish legitimate implementation branches and different observed objects. Explain changed_premise and affected_uses; preserve old revisions and receipts, leave unrelated uses untouched. The controller surfaces explicit uses, not inferred contradictions. Answer challenged premises with sources or compatible observations; rerun only changed experiment bases.

After a local answer, pause or unchanged update, reconsider the whole A1/A2 space. Choose any valuable unrelated sourced question, or follow a newly grounded local lead as a separate question when its responsibility is independent. Neither a parent link nor old knowledge use is mandatory. Prior understanding enriches but does not restrict this choice; no forced alternation or coverage quota. explained means locally answered, including a positive defect, not globally finished. Investigate tractable producer, prefix, schedule and endpoint gaps; a child answer does not close its parent. Keep pause resume conditions and open-run continuity. With little time, a source trace or honest unfinished discriminator can be useful. No empty handoffs or compulsory tests; human review owns final CFT classification.
