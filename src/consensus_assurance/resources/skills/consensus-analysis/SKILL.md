# Consensus investigation

Use consensus formation/progress and context/authority transitions as complementary lines: how decisions become justified and accepted duties reach completion, cancellation or transfer, and how their basis survives authority acquisition, transfer or loss. Membership, persistence, recovery, asynchronous work and client contracts connect them. Other Activities support investigation, not mandatory labels or stages. Broad orientation and local traces refine one another; a sourced question or probe needs no global map, principal Fact or settled requirement.

A Behavior describes actual conditional execution: guards, ordering, state changes, messages, returns and completion. Preserve materially different failure, visibility, ownership, persistence and asynchronous paths. A Fact states what those behaviors establish for an identified object and context under explicit conditions. Distinguish required behavior, source assertions and individual observations; neither vacuous True nor an expectation copied from documentation is a useful Fact.

Start from a concrete decision or pending duty and trace its producer, consumer and a real event that may change their shared conditions. A provisional hypothesis and discriminator can identify what the current explanation predicts and which source or observation would change it. Trace admission, serialization, reset and completion; omitted work can reveal a lost duty without a wrong decision. Names, guards, asymmetry and existing tests do not prove eligibility, freshness or same-operation association. Missing normative support can itself be investigated; current behavior or a TODO does not establish a universal contract. Source-explained negatives need equal care. Reading, construction and repair can share a turn, and source reasoning can settle a suspicion without testing. Use evidence-review for applicable responsibilities and observed endpoints, not an exhaustive checklist or private chain of thought.

Promote Behavior/Fact knowledge when reused or needing durable correction, with sources, observations and conditions. Record the exact entry revision and relied-on assertion for actual uses, not shared vocabulary. When evidence changes a saved premise, revise its existing id; a new Fact cannot substitute for correction. Inspect recorded direct and derived uses, including older revisions, and identify affected terminal investigations, experiments or interpretations in affected_uses with changed_premise. Reliance is not automatic invalidity: preserve independent support, unrelated uses, old revisions and receipts. Answer challenged premises in their original investigation; unchanged receipts can support a corrected interpretation.

After a local answer, pause or unchanged update, return to A1/A2 with the revised understanding. Which sourced relationship or condition could change the current explanation? A protection or recovery path may expose a partial failure, a different producer, or queued work after authority change. Coverage suggests locations, not independent defect questions or guarantees; reading all files does not exhaust their relationships. Investigate tractable producer, prefix, schedule and endpoint gaps. Keep one investigation for variants of the same cause and responsibility; refutation is useful, local answers do not settle parents, and pause conditions still apply. Choose useful work using actual allocation; timeouts are ceilings, not predicted costs or a closing phase. With little time, a source trace or honest unfinished discriminator can suffice. No forced alternation, tests, knowledge-use quotas, invented suspicions or repeated closing handoffs.
