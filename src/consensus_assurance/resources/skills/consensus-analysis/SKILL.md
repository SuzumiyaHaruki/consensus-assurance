# Consensus investigation

Use consensus formation/progress and context/authority transitions as complementary lines: how decisions and completions become justified, and how that justification survives authority acquisition, transfer or loss. Membership, persistence, recovery, asynchronous work and client contracts connect them. Other Activities provide implementation support, not mandatory labels or stages. Broad orientation and local traces refine one another; a sourced local question or probe needs no global map, principal Fact or settled requirement.

A Behavior describes actual conditional execution: guards, ordering, state changes, messages, returns and completion. Use the coarsest granularity preserving materially different failure, visibility, ownership, persistence and asynchronous paths. A Fact expresses what those behaviors establish for an identified object and context under explicit conditions. Required behavior, code-established assertions and one execution's observations differ. A source assertion may precede testing; an observed instance is not a universal guarantee. Neither vacuous True nor an expected property copied from documentation is a useful Fact.

Trace what producers establish, transmit and retain and what consumers rely on, including intervening admission, serialization, reset and completion duties. Asymmetry alone is not a defect; guards, thresholds, names and existing tests do not prove freshness, eligibility or same-operation association. A short hypothesis and discriminator should identify the applicable responsibility, apparent mismatch and observation or enclosing protection that would refute it. Source-explained negatives need equal care. Missing normative support can be investigated; current behavior or a TODO does not create a universal contract. Do not disclose private chain of thought or fill an exhaustive checklist.

Two loops interact: orientation and local understanding; question, witness and missing-link investigation. Source relationship → question → probe → missing premise → supported answer → knowledge correction/reuse → next question is a causal trajectory, not required product stages. Reading, construction and repair can continue within one turn. An explanation may settle a suspicion without testing; a local interface result need not demonstrate the worst distributed consequence.

Promote Behavior/Fact knowledge when reused, relevant to another question or needing durable correction. Preserve sources/observations and conditions; uses identify entry revision and, when needed, the particular assertion relied upon. Scratch notes are not authoritative. Corrections explain the changed premise and identify affected recorded uses; unrelated additions, formatting and shared vocabulary do not invalidate evidence. One explanation may address several shared uses. The controller preserves old bases and surfaces uses, but cannot infer semantic relevance from text similarity. Answer challenged premises with sources or compatible observations; rerun only when the experiment basis changes.

Investigate tractable producer, prefix, schedule and endpoint gaps. An answered child does not close its parent. A local pause records concrete resume conditions without ending an open run. No fixed numbers of facts, reviews, tests or findings; no repeated unchanged handoffs to consume budget. Human review owns final CFT classification and root-cause scoring.
