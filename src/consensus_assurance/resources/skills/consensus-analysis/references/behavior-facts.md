# Conditions, effects and semantic facts

Describe sourced owners, triggers, guards, state changes, effects and asynchronous boundaries. For a relevant relationship already read, identify who establishes the fact under which conditions and what its consumer actually requires. Include other read establishment, invalidation or reinterpretation paths; unread paths remain unknown or sourced Surfaces.

Shared updates do not imply shared follow-up effects. When one entry establishes a fact and schedules a consumer but another only establishes the fact, preserve that distinction in trigger, important_branches, writes, external_effects or async_boundaries. Do not union effects into an unconditional guarantee. Distinguish possible effects, conditional scheduling and guaranteed completion. A Behavior can cover multiple entries when conditions remain clear; split minimally when owners, completion boundaries or meanings cannot be expressed accurately together. Do not enumerate every branch or duplicate a shared Fact.

Fact states the assertion actually established for its identity and validity_context, not the strongest effect seen on any path. Reporting, internal accounting, notification and completed external work are distinct. Trace relevant guard inputs to their producer, update point, owner and completion duty instead of trusting a variable name. Conversely, a missing local field is not a missing guarantee when dispatch, admission, serialization or reset supplies it. Assess the composed producer/consumer protection, not each structure in isolation. Absent direct action is a question: inspect alternative consumers, delayed validation, shared scheduling and recovery for the same object and history before alleging a defect.

Use produces_fact_ids/consumes_fact_ids for established relationships needed by later questions; reverse indexes are derived. No name-based edges, mandatory edges, branch fields or completeness scores. Reference validation proves structure, not equivalent path meaning. Guards and observed differences do not supply normative obligations without applicable sources and reasoning.

Use existing map_changes at natural handoffs. Correcting an overstrong abstraction challenges the specific Candidate premise that depended on it; independent results retain their original basis. Use preserves only when requirements, dependencies and observation scope actually remain applicable. Meaning, identity, context and dependency changes cannot masquerade as clarification. No change means no version; keep history and counterevidence.

Keep one primary home for each kind of knowledge: Behavior owns producer conditions, changes, synchronization, notification and exit mechanisms; Fact owns the assertion for its identity and validity_context; Candidate/feedback owns the question's answer, actual execution and remaining discriminator; Activity/core_overview owns the core connections, higher-level understanding and navigation; Surface owns unread entries and reasons; CheckRun/assessment/review owns experiment progress and judgments. Scope owns exclusions. Preserve substantive overviews without copying the same long answer and residuals through every layer. Mapped means association, not resolution. An explained Candidate needs no challenge merely to restate its protection; changed protection or Fact context does require attribution. Use the guide's natural handoff for answered unknowns and tasks/audit.md for Material and execution references.
