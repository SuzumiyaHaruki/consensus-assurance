Declare legal inputs, actual controls and observation adaptations for this check's endpoint. A local comparison need not establish unrelated deployment consequences. REACHABILITY Separate the requirement's source and applicability, the consumer effect under controlled input, actual producer execution and same-history correlation, and the observed system endpoint. A call edge or constructible input does not execute its producer. Two isolated fragments do not establish one legal protocol history. Name substituted edges, fault assumptions and identity/context/generation continuity. A blocked checker with a concrete encoding correction should finish its existing bounded unit before unrelated discovery, subject to the same budgets. An old checker issue remains open until a new artifact actually executes and a focused correspondence review resolves that issue; a permitted pending path cannot be called a safety violation merely because an equality oracle expected success.
