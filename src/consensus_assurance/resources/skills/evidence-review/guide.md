SEMANTIC REVIEW Use the supplied review contract against actual fixed inputs, source dependencies and observations. Direct Check requires one checker_correspondence review covering the applicable requirement, selected entry and caller duties, oracle, identity, legal history, observation completion and contrary evidence. Applicability and decomposition are separate questions, not mandatory extra essays: include them when a concrete issue needs that answer. Seek weaker/stronger contracts, alternative mechanisms and unmet dependencies. For models, inspect behavior and checker separately, including trigger reachability and excluded branches. No issue found is provisional, neither a proof nor an independent vote.

CURRENT VERSION Cite unchanged established parts and focus on the increment: what changed, which premise it answers, what the new execution observed and what remains unanswered. New driver inputs require their own actual execution and correspondence; old review success cannot certify them. Scope/cause/consequence questions outside the checked proposition are independent limits, not additional confirmation gates. Never alter a requirement or weaken a predicate merely to obtain a passing test.

ISSUES Fixed Claim, Harness and Monitor conflicts have exact issue/condition records. Address the listed issue with a sourced resolution and, where supplied, condition dispositions; do not answer the same conflict separately in several products. A no_issue_found item alone clears nothing. Preserve current counterevidence as an open issue, explained alternatives in rationale and independent limits in limitations. Resolution applies to its answering version and descendants; it does not legalize an older driver. Shared semantic issues follow actual dependencies, not checker names.

REPAIR Distinguish review aspect from challenged component. Driver/configuration/initialization changes preserve the requirement and oracle, replace only sourced current premises, and need fresh execution. Changed observation/oracle encoding uses explicit correction; changed normative meaning/applicability uses F2, and proposition-preserving dependency expansion uses F3. Resolve against the actual changed component, never an unrelated edit. If additional source evidence answers the issue without changing inputs, resolve on that executed version instead of manufacturing a harness rewrite. Remaining issues and the current version's missing correspondence stay visible independently.
