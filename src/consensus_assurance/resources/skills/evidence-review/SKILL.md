# Execution-grounded interpretation

Ground conclusions in applicable responsibilities, necessary history and actual contradictable endpoints. A comparison evaluates its declared predicate: holds means satisfied in applicable observations, violated means contradicted by a valid witness, unknown means undecided. A successful anomaly prediction can return holds while supporting a defect explanation. Exploration need not first settle a correctness duty or invert its prediction. Keep predicate meaning and the model's scoped defect_claim separate: execution success, comparison results and structural acceptance do not establish a defect or independent human verification. Review sources, fixed inputs and receipts, not approval votes.

Distinguish guarantees from recommendations, probabilistic descriptions, implementation behavior and individual test expectations; names, guards or a TODO do not establish a universal contract. When responsibility is disputed, reread the full defining contract context, distinguishing caller duties, implementation guarantees and observed branches. Identify who establishes a necessary premise: the caller, environment or target's producer. Prohibited external use alone does not establish a defect; an internal producer violating its consumer's premise under permitted use remains a target responsibility. Prefer normal initialization and actual producers for protocol-dependent claims. Real producers constrain event origin; controlled delivery and processing must stay within the target fault model and preserve actual transport, ordering, cancellation and RPC lifecycle semantics. Constructors, test facilities, logical ticks, memory transports and storage can suffice, including multiple real instances in one process. Direct handlers and supplied state remain useful exploration: replace consequential supplied premises with real prefixes, find enclosing protections, justify an independently applicable local contract, or retain the gap. Another consumer of the same invented state is not reachability progress.

Reused prefixes/checkpoints must preserve relevant identity, membership, ordering, persistence and pending work. Disk bytes do not recreate replies, workers or futures; matching numbers do not make fragments one compatible history. Justify particular omissions, executing the prefix when easier. Match fault assumptions to events: a failing-write window is not one failed call, nor an allowed store error corruption of an acknowledged durable write. Initialization does not legalize later inputs, and API acceptance does not prove caller permission. A local endpoint cannot waive necessary calling/history conditions or settle broader consequences.

Trace decisive observations from the actual runtime object through callbacks or returns to sampling and completion; adjacent source statements do not establish atomicity. Check identity, actual returned values, operands, predicate and operation order against harness and raw output, not scenario names. When a decisive proxy is ambiguous or observations conflict, inspect their computation and sampling before rerunning or expanding the scenario. Prefer existing independent observations or sources that distinguish concrete rival explanations; negative measurements must distinguish the hypothesis under the stated conditions. Derived booleans need inspectable operands; constants are not observed triggers. Before/after samples can miss intermediate transitions; zero detections alone establish neither absence nor an occurrence-rate bound. Resolve consequential sampling ambiguity through synchronization, ownership or a check that the observer detects the relevant transition. Log entries and quorum counters are not FSM effects; calls are not completions. Missing data, including an unobserved implication antecedent, remains unknown: retain the result event, independent identity and sampling failure reason, but omit unobserved compared fields rather than defaulting them to false, zero or an empty string. JSON null is a value, not a missing-field marker. Preserve the actual endpoint and timing: output timestamps need not mark the underlying event.

A bounded progress defect can be supported by a legal trigger and actual authority/producer prefix, repeated nonprogress under stated fair/normal opportunities, and a source-supported retry/reject/reset cycle with no enabled repair under those conditions. State the actual interval, mechanism and environmental/fault assumptions. A short wait, artificial timeout or isolated stalled goroutine without this basis is insufficient; a supported scoped defect needs neither infinite execution nor proof against every future repair. Controls should distinguish concrete competing causes with consistent sampling and comparison where practical. If the result persists without its proposed cause, investigate the harness and predicate. No fixed control quota is required; decisive observations or sourced explanations can suffice. Never modify production logic to manufacture evidence.

Identity must be independent of compared values; do not identify responses by potentially corrupted context or filter applicability on expected results. Each comparison requires its admission and referenced prerequisite chains, not alternative scenario admissions. Preserve missing completions, contradictory same-operation observations and parse loss. A complete witness may survive an independent unfinished scenario, never missing same-witness history.

Let consequential counterevidence change the original question's explanation. Determine whether it defeats a necessary premise, limits the consequence, or excludes only one trigger or competing cause; the last does not by itself settle the original question or its parent. A calling restriction or protection defeating the premise requires correction, narrowing or refutation; listing it as a limitation does not preserve the claim. Retention needs sourced reasons it does not apply; narrowing needs its own applicable duty. Otherwise retain an unresolved claim and investigate that premise rather than reproducing the disputed symptom. A successful path only settles what it establishes; retain and investigate concrete unobserved failure, producer or completion paths. Keep controls, excluded causes and superseded failures. Necessary gaps affect applicability, legal history, observation validity or cause and remain until specifically answered; broader impact can be separate when the local duty stands independently. Cite exact target CheckRun IDs and zero-based parsed indices, linking separate producer receipts with joint_history. The controller validates recorded identity and answers, not arbitrary semantic sufficiency.

Interpretation owns current findings, counterevidence and limits, including source-only accounts before execution. Its latest version covers the whole question, focused on current evidence and substantive boundaries; process bookkeeping and superseded accounts remain in retained logs and history. Compare a revised account with prior conditions and counterevidence; explain what new evidence or source understanding supports the change, retaining still-applicable findings and boundaries with references. Correct interpretations that depended on invalid sampling while preserving independent observations. Stronger consequences need their own support, not just repeated observations. Reuse unchanged receipts to correct an explanation without recopying history or rerunning for labels; exploration without a fixed requirement can support later sourced interpretation while its original basis remains intact. Changed requirement, applicability, harness, observation or comparison needs a successor with the actual difference and fresh execution. Read existing outputs first; repeats can examine reproducibility or schedule variation, but cannot establish that the measurement means what its label claims.

Build/setup failure, cancellation and timeout cannot become completed evidence. A completed violating witness followed by independent test failure is a narrow exception: cite the receipt, comparison/witness indices, output and harness ranges, explaining why completion and sampling do not depend on the failure. Other failed receipts can support explained/unresolved/conditional accounts without claiming a completed comparison. Do not delete assertions or rerun unchanged inputs merely to obtain success. Preserve concrete limits without repetitive generic caveats.
