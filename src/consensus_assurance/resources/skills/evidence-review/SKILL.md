name: evidence-review
description: Interpret observations against applicable contracts and executable history.
---

Distinguish three evidence claims in reasoning, without a new classification form. A conditional observation establishes what fixed code did given explicitly supplied state/input. A local contract violation needs a sourced contract that actually admits that input/initial state, actual behavior and a matching oracle; it need not prove a whole-cluster consequence. A reachable protocol violation needs one permitted common history producing the necessary participants, state, inputs and context through the actual endpoint. Calling a private handler or real constructor does not establish its later supplied premises. A narrower endpoint alone changes none of those preconditions.

Before no_issue_found, for holds and violations, explain which written-obligation premises apply and how fixed execution, real producers/callers and observations establish them, or why a sourced local contract independently admits them. Close a current-premise issue by compatible actual evidence (fixed artifact, exact CheckRun, decisive observations and prefix), or specific source reasoning showing the objection was mistaken. Existing sources and unchanged inputs can suffice. A genuinely different local responsibility uses a successor/linked investigation and leaves the original question visible. An unresolved condition is independent scope only if it cannot change this claim's applicability, necessary-history legality or observation validity. One bounded schedule need not cover all deployments; a missing necessary producer or joint history is not that kind of limitation. Retain the measurement and investigate its concrete gap.

Inspect obligation, predicate and observation separately. Observe actual values from the same operation with coherent sampling/order justified by control flow, synchronization and ownership. Check producer, caller, waiter and completion/cancellation duties. Event labels, intended inputs, sleeps, counters and entry presence do not prove completion. Preserve fault/transport contracts; cleanup and process success cannot supply an endpoint or history.

Harness prerequisites name observed conditions. Monitor.admission_alias identifies admitted operations whose completion is owed; explicit alias references establish causal predecessors, not array order. Correlate input/result using independent identity, never the compared value or a changing context. Do not repeat results as prerequisites or filter applicability on the result. Missing identity/completion, parse loss, unreached setup and unsupported temporal expressions stay gaps. A complete local witness may coexist with missing independent operations, never same-witness ambiguity. For S implies E, independently observe S and E; S=false is bounded non-occurrence, not proof of E, and missing is not false.

Keep each necessary premise once: contracts in claim grounding/scope; produced history in harness legality and observed prerequisites; unresolved/conflicting premises in their owning grounding and issue. Reference accepted claims/bindings instead of copying notes to monitors. Independent extensions belong in scope, uncertainties or limitations; next discriminators in Candidate/feedback. Moving a note or generic approval resolves nothing: answer its issue ID with sources/reasoning, preserving other issues. The controller verifies identities, versions, associations and observations, not reachability from prose.

Review fixed bytes and exact CheckRuns. Changed harness, oracle, applicability, observation or claim uses a successor, fresh execution and review; old results never transfer to changed inputs. A pure interpretation correction may reuse an unchanged execution and already-held sources, preserving the prior opinion. Repair only what the evidence requires: adding independent identity need not alter an already correct comparison. No component-touch or novel-citation requirement applies. Exploration remains conditional evidence, not retroactive formal confirmation.

A normal nonzero test exit can retain a completed violating witness only through execution_attribution on checker_correspondence: exact CheckRun, checker IDs and valid parsed event indices, retained failure stream and fixed harness line ranges. Justify from actual control flow why every duty needed for that witness completed before an independent failure. Machine validation cannot prove causal independence; stdout/stderr line numbers are not a shared clock. This exception never supplies holds or excuses timeout, cancellation, signal, build failure, missing premises or contradictory observations. Preserve failure and other scenario debt; do not stop workers or omit required joins after printing a result.

Review is provisional internal reasoning, not proof or an independent vote. Keep contrary evidence, source disagreements and independent limits distinct. Optional Chinese summaries describe observations and boundaries without changing verdicts or requiring a translation turn. Final CFT classification and root-cause deduplication remain offline human work.
