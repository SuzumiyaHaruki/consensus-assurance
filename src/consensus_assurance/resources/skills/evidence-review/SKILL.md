# Execution-grounded interpretation

Ground conclusions in applicable responsibilities, necessary history and actual contradictable endpoints. A comparison evaluates its declared predicate: holds means satisfied in applicable observations, violated means contradicted by a valid witness, unknown means undecided. A successful anomaly prediction can return holds while supporting a defect explanation. Exploration need not first settle a correctness duty or invert its prediction. Keep predicate meaning and the model's scoped defect_claim separate: execution success, comparison results and structural acceptance do not establish a defect or independent human verification. Review sources, fixed inputs and receipts, not approval votes.

Distinguish guarantees from recommendations, probabilistic descriptions, implementation behavior and individual test expectations; names, guards or a TODO do not establish a universal contract. When responsibility is disputed, reread the defining contract, distinguishing caller duties, guarantees and observed branches. Identify who establishes a necessary premise: caller, environment or target producer. Prohibited external use alone does not establish a defect; an internal producer violating its consumer's premise under permitted use remains a target responsibility. Trace the decisive input's permitted origin: real surrounding nodes, valid fields and API acceptance do not establish it. Controlled delivery and processing must preserve the target fault model and actual transport, ordering, cancellation and RPC lifecycle semantics. Constructors, test facilities, logical ticks, memory transports and storage can suffice, including multiple real instances in one process. Direct handlers and supplied state remain useful exploration: replace consequential supplied premises with real prefixes, find enclosing protections, justify an independently applicable local contract, or retain the gap. Another consumer of the same invented state is not reachability progress.

Reused prefixes/checkpoints must preserve relevant identity, membership, ordering, persistence and pending work, including caller duties omitted by reused helpers. Progress markers do not establish completion of those duties. Disk bytes do not recreate replies, workers or futures; matching numbers do not make fragments one compatible history. Replaying or shrinking a retained action prefix can locate the first conflict between construction and source explanations; preserve relevant preconditions, since a seed alone may not preserve action choices. Match fault assumptions to events: a failing-write window is not one failed call, nor an allowed store error corruption of an acknowledged durable write. A local endpoint cannot waive necessary calling/history conditions or settle broader consequences.

Relate decisive observations to the events, objects and times actually measured by fixed code and receipts, then to the sourced responsibility and claim they support. Trace the necessary intermediate inferences through producers, consumers, callbacks, returns and sampling; labels and adjacent statements establish neither these relations nor atomicity. Distinguish return representation from application content, log/quorum state from FSM effects, and invocation from completion. Check whether normal processing or a failure/default-return path explains the observation. Separate source-supported unexecuted paths from measurements. If a necessary link lacks support, retain the established behavior and narrow the claim or leave that link unresolved.

For a disputed or strengthened inference, use source, saved observations or a focused experiment to distinguish concrete permitted explanations. An intervention must reach the relevant operation and change or isolate the competing cause, not merely a proxy parameter. Persistence after removing that cause challenges its explanation. Compared executions must reach the relevant exposure or phase with compatible sampling. Align the same object's pre-operation state and subsequent change; an already-present outcome does not establish that operation's effect. Before/after samples may miss transitions; zero detections establish neither absence nor a rate bound. Resolve consequential sampling ambiguity through synchronization, ownership or checking the observer's ability to detect the transition, without a control quota. Preserve invocation/completion intervals; output timestamps need not mark the underlying event. Derived booleans need inspectable operands; constants are not observed triggers. Missing data, including an unobserved antecedent, stays unknown: retain the result event, independent identity and sampling failure reason, omitting unobserved compared fields rather than defaulting to false, zero or an empty string. JSON null is a value, not a missing-field marker.

A bounded progress defect can be supported by a legal trigger and actual authority/producer prefix, repeated nonprogress under stated fair/normal opportunities, and a source-supported retry/reject/reset cycle with no enabled repair under those conditions. State the actual interval, mechanism and environmental/fault assumptions. A short wait, artificial timeout or isolated stalled goroutine without this basis is insufficient; a supported scoped defect needs neither infinite execution nor proof against every future repair. Never modify production logic to manufacture evidence.

Identity must be independent of compared values; do not identify responses by potentially corrupted context or filter applicability on expected results. Each comparison requires its admission and referenced prerequisite chains, not alternative scenario admissions. Preserve missing completions, contradictory same-operation observations and parse loss. A complete witness may survive an independent unfinished scenario, never missing same-witness history.

Distinguish three negative results: invalid construction or measurement removes a witness's support, not the original suspicion; a valid execution without the consequence supports a negative only within its exercised conditions and observation range; a sourced protection or argument covering the claimed scope can refute or narrow the suspicion there. A broader exclusion must cover the permitted variations and object/timing relationships it depends on; excluding one trigger or cause does not settle the question or its parent. Sources or existing evidence can suffice without another test.

Let consequential counterevidence change the original explanation. A calling restriction or protection defeating a necessary premise requires correction, narrowing or refutation; listing it as a limitation does not preserve the claim. Retention needs sourced reasons the counterevidence does not apply; narrowing needs its own applicable duty. Otherwise investigate the unresolved premise rather than repeat the disputed symptom. Keep controls, excluded causes, superseded failures and concrete unobserved failure, producer or completion paths. Necessary gaps affect applicability, legal history, observation validity or cause and remain until specifically answered; broader impact can be separate when the local duty stands independently. Cite exact target CheckRun IDs and zero-based parsed indices, linking separate producer receipts with joint_history. The controller validates recorded identity and answers, not arbitrary semantic sufficiency.

Interpretation independently expresses the question's current claim, decisive basis, important counterevidence and remaining limits, including before execution. When evidence changes the judgment, replace the affected argument; remove invalid current reasoning rather than appending a retraction beneath it. Correct known knowledge and uses that depend on the changed premise, identified by sources, receipt IDs and observations, not scenario similarity. Preserve independent support and its conditions; neither merge executions into an unsupported history nor withdraw a witness because another failed. References locate review sites, not automatic invalidation; check retained claims against their original basis. Consolidate reproductions that leave the judgment unchanged without dropping conditions, contradictory results or unresolved scope. Earlier explanations and individual executions remain in history and receipts; the current account need not retell them. AuditSubmission.rationale explains the update.

Correct interpretations of unchanged evidence without rerunning; exploration without a fixed requirement can support later sourced interpretation while retaining its original basis. Abandoned tests need not all be repaired. Changed requirement, applicability, harness, observation or comparison needs a successor recording the actual difference and fresh execution. Repetition alone establishes neither schedule independence nor measurement validity. A changed occurrence rate suggests hidden conditions; it does not negate a valid witness unless further investigation invalidates that witness or a necessary premise.

Build/setup failure, cancellation and timeout cannot become completed evidence. A completed violating witness alongside independent test failure is a narrow exception: cite the receipt, comparison/witness indices, output and harness ranges, explaining why its premises, completion and sampling do not depend on the failure; test names or execution order alone do not establish independence. Other failed receipts can support explained/unresolved/conditional accounts without claiming a completed comparison. Do not delete assertions or rerun unchanged inputs merely to obtain success. Preserve concrete limits without repetitive generic caveats.
