---
name: evidence-review
description: Constrain each interpretation by applicable responsibility, actual history and actual observations.
---

For the current conclusion ask: (1) what sourced contract/responsibility applies under which conditions; (2) how necessary state, inputs and identities arose in one permitted execution; (3) what the unmodified implementation actually did at the claimed endpoint. These are dependencies, not mandatory stages or a repeated form. Before no_issue_found, for holds as well as violations, establish the written obligation's premises from fixed inputs, real producers/callers and observed history, or show why a sourced local interface permits the input/initial state. An unresolved condition is independent scope only if it cannot change applicability, required-history legality or observation validity. Otherwise retain the measurement and its concrete gap; investigate, dispute or revise it.

Inspect the obligation, encoded predicate and actual observation separately. Observe decisive values from the actual object/message and same operation; event labels, intended inputs, quorum counters or entry presence do not prove completion or applied effects. Establish order and coherent sampling through the implementation's control flow, synchronization and ownership. Sleep is not readiness, a printed flag is not a witness, a call site is not callee semantics. Check producer, caller, waiter, completion/cancellation owner and relevant async boundary. Preserve the fault model: a permitted failed write differs from rewriting an already durable value. Cleanup and successful process exit cannot substitute for the endpoint or required history.

Harness prerequisites name observed conditions; Monitor.admission_alias names the prerequisite admitting the operations whose completion is owed. Explicit alias references establish causal predecessors, not array order. Correlate input and result with independent identity, never the compared value or a context expected to change. Do not repeat results as prerequisites or filter applicability on the result. Missing/ambiguous identity, missing completion, parse loss, unreached setup and unsupported temporal expressions remain gaps. A complete local violating witness may coexist with missing results of independent operations; same-witness ambiguity cannot. For S implies E, independently observe S and E: S=false is bounded non-occurrence here, not proof of E; missing observations are not false.

Review fixed bytes and exact CheckRuns. Exploration guides construction and cannot be retroactively promoted to formal confirmation. Changed harness, oracle, assumptions, observation or claim uses revise_check, its predecessor and reason, then fresh execution and correspondence review. The controller records actual changes and preserves earlier bytes, requirements, outputs and reviews. A knowledge correction alone does not demand rerunning an unaffected check. Resolve an exact open issue with its supporting sources/execution; no_issue_found alone cancels neither registered counterevidence nor a necessary unresolved premise. Old failures remain visible, and a resolution for a successor does not legalize the predecessor. A narrower claim does not settle the original consequence.

A normal nonzero test exit can retain a completed violating witness only through execution_attribution on checker_correspondence: exact CheckRun, checker IDs and valid parsed event indices, retained failure stream line range and fixed harness line range. Explain from sources and observed control flow why every duty needed for this witness completed before an independent failure. Machine checks verify eligible records, not causal independence; stdout/stderr line numbers are not a shared clock. This exception never supplies holds or excuses timeout, cancellation, signal, build failure, missing premises or contradictory observations. Keep failure and other scenario debt; do not kill workers or drop required joins after printing an expected result.

A no-issue review is provisional internal investigation, not proof or an independent vote. Keep contrary evidence and source disagreements distinct from an implementation/requirement discrepancy. Explain independent scope limits without promoting a local result to whole-system safety. Optional Chinese result summaries describe observations and limitations; they do not change verdicts and require no translation turn.
