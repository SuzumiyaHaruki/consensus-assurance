DISCOVERY METHOD
Keep semantic goals meaningful: a goal may cover several obligations and need not be reduced to a function-level assertion. Separately choose a small executable audit unit. During system survey consider distinct responsibilities and their actual interactions; only at local model selection prefer one tractable obligation while preserving others as pending work.
1. Identify an externally significant event and the responsibilities its consumers assume. Locate the producer, consumer, and the data or completion signal they exchange.
2. Derive a candidate obligation from that dependency. Attribute observations separately from expected responsibilities. Seek alternate mechanisms, weaker configured contracts, and conflicting evidence before treating the candidate as applicable.
3. Locate actual definitions and branch ranges; use one literal symbol per binding. An entire file is reading coverage, not a precise behavioral explanation.
4. Identify unknown inputs, writers, recovery paths, and delayed work crossing the unit boundary. Request the smallest actual ranges that resolve a named uncertainty. State how the answer changes the next action; do not replace missing code with protocol memory.
5. Select using significance, available evidence, and remaining budget. Do not attempt all obligations merely to complete a large graph. Report deferred obligations and joint dependencies in gaps. A scoped checker cannot discharge a whole goal.

ACTIONABLE AUDIT QUESTIONS
Each new audit unit needs an audit_question stating its importance, actual material sources, participants, objects, semantic contexts and event paths where known. Suspicious code or a historical bug is not required. Record applicable establishment, maintenance, use, recovery or handoff points with source and binding references and explicit unknowns. Do not mechanically fill every category. Important responsibility handoffs need linked units, a sourced reason no separate check is needed, or a bounded inquiry even when both responsibilities already have goals. Prefer important uncovered interactions, avoid duplicate coverage, and account for executability and budget.
Round and phase meanings come from current implementation evidence. They are not global barriers; do not assume synchronized nodes, empty channels at round end, or one model per phase. A legitimate alternative mechanism may discharge a responsibility without a familiar guard. The supplied overview is partial; request missing actual ranges through reading requests.
