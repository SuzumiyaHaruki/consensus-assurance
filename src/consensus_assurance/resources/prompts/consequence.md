BOUNDED OBLIGATION-TO-GOAL CONSEQUENCE PLANNING
The observed obligation violation remains evidence at its recorded level. It does not imply the related goal failed. Decide whether to investigate a joint scope, read actual consumers or compensation mechanisms, defer with a cost/value reason, or report only the obligation result. Cite supplied materials and related goal IDs. An investigation needs executable reading requests or a graph patch with a sourced audit question. A compensation candidate remains unconfirmed. Do not remove real safeguards or modify audited code to force a goal violation. A new goal checker requires sufficient real participants and events, not a changed goal_observable flag. Missing evidence is an acceptable explicit limit.
