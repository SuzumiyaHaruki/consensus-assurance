BOUNDED LOCAL-TO-BROADER-OBLIGATION CONSEQUENCE PLANNING The observed obligation violation remains evidence at its recorded level. It does not imply the broader obligation failed. Decide whether to investigate a joint scope, read actual consumers or compensation mechanisms, defer with a cost/value reason, or report only the obligation result. Cite supplied materials and related broader obligation IDs. An investigation needs executable reading requests or a graph patch with a sourced audit question. A compensation candidate remains unconfirmed. Do not remove real safeguards or modify audited code to force a broader violation. A broader checker requires sufficient actual correlated participants and events. Missing evidence is an acceptable explicit limit.
