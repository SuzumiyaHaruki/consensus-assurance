Conclusion: violated. With RestoreCommittedLogs enabled, NewRaft does not complete a legal replay of already-committed log entries once the replay needs more than 128 FSM dispatch batches; the caller never receives the *Raft, so the node has no way to start, serve or shut down. This is a startup progress failure, not a decision-safety failure: no commit, vote or membership decision was observed to be wrong. Evidence. Mechanism from source: api.go:606 calls restoreFromCommittedLogs before any background goroutine exists; api.go:753 calls processLogs(commitIndex, nil); raft.go:1303-1313 sends each commitTuple batch into fsmMutateCh and selects only on that send and on r.shutdownCh; fsmMutateCh has capacity 128 (api.go:560); its only reader runFSM (fsm.go:241) is started at api.go:637 after NewRaft returns; api.go:1065 is the only closer of shutdownCh and is reachable only through a *Raft. After 128 successful sends the 129th send therefore has no producer and no possible closer, so the block is permanent rather than slow. Consequence under the exercised conditions: a node restarting with the documented RestoreCommittedLogs option and with more than 128*MaxAppendEntries committed entries beyond its last snapshot hangs inside NewRaft indefinitely while reporting no error.
