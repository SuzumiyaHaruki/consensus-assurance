# Consensus investigation

A Behavior describes actual conditional execution: guards, ordering, state changes, messages, returns and completion. A Fact expresses what those behaviors establish for an identified object and context under explicit conditions. Required behavior, code-established assertions and one execution's observations differ. A source assertion may precede testing; an observed instance is not a universal guarantee. Neither vacuous True nor an expected property copied from documentation is a useful Fact.

Trace what producers establish, transmit and retain and what consumers rely on, including intervening admission, serialization, reset and completion duties. # Execution-grounded interpretation

Ground engineering conclusions in applicable responsibilities, necessary history and actual contradictable endpoints. A comparison evaluates its declared predicate: holds means satisfied in the applicable observations, violated means contradicted by a valid witness, unknown means undecided. A successful anomaly prediction may return holds while supporting a defect explanation; a justified correctness duty may use a predicate that faulty behavior contradicts. Exploration need not first settle that duty or invert its prediction to be useful. Preserve predicate meaning, history and measured operands; interpret engineering significance separately. Review applicability and correspondence against sources, fixed inputs and receipts, not approval votes.

For protocol-dependent claims prefer normal initialization and actual producers, controlling a few permitted environmental events. Reuse constructors, test facilities, logical ticks, memory transports and storage. One process with multiple real instances suffices when relevant contracts hold. Direct handlers and state injection remain useful exploration; accepting pre-filled storage does not establish its producer history. After a conditional anomaly, replace the consequential supplied premise with a real prefix, find an enclosing protection, justify an independently applicable local contract, or retain the gap. Another consumer of the same invented state is not reachability progress.

Reused prefixes/checkpoints must preserve relevant identity, membership, ordering, persistence and pending work. Disk bytes do not recreate replies, workers or futures. Separately valid fragments are not one compatible history merely because numbers match. Source-justified reductions must explain the particular omission; execute the prefix when easier. Match fault assumptions to events: a failing-write window is not one failed call; an allowed store error is not corruption of an acknowledged durable write. Initialization does not legalize later inputs; API acceptance does not prove caller permission. A local endpoint cannot waive necessary calling/history conditions or settle an unresolved broader consequence.

Inspect sampling, raw operands and predicate together. Measure actual identity, output, errors and coherent before/after state using synchronization or justified ownership. Derived booleans need inspectable operands and computation; a scenario constant is not an observed trigger. Log entries or quorum counters are not FSM effects; calls are not completions. Missing data, including an unobserved implication antecedent, is unknown rather than false or zero. No keyword scan, boolean ban or constructor whitelist establishes measurement validity.

A reachable progress/liveness defect can be supported by a legal trigger and actual authority/producer prefix, repeated bounded nonprogress under stated fair/normal opportunities, and a source-supported retry/reject/reset cycle restoring the blocking state with no enabled repair under those conditions. Use controls or competing-cause discrimination where material. State the actual observation interval, mechanism and environmental/fault assumptions. A short wait, artificial timeout, unobserved completion or isolated stalled goroutine without such a mechanism is insufficient. Conversely, recognizing a concrete scoped defect does not require infinite execution, model checking, every configuration or proof against every future external repair. A derived stalled flag alone cannot certify it; do not downgrade a supported bounded argument to mere suspicion.

Identity must be independent of the compared value; do not identify responses by potentially corrupted context or filter applicability on the expected result. Each comparison requires its admission and explicitly referenced prerequisite chains, not alternative scenario admissions. Preserve missing completions, ambiguous/contradictory same-operation observations and parse loss. A complete witness may survive an independent unfinished scenario, never missing same-witness history.

Prefer fault-free, changed-schedule or other legal-input controls that can refute the explanation, using the same sampling/comparison where practical. If the result persists without its proposed cause, investigate the harness and predicate. This is not a two-test quota: a decisive identity/byte comparison or sourced explanation may suffice. Never modify production logic to manufacture evidence. Review the weakest necessary link and let it change the next action without repetitive essays.

A registered necessary gap blocks its affected judgment until answered with relevant execution or a sourced correction. A contrary result undermining the current proposition needs such a gap, a narrower/revised claim, or a conditional/unresolved interpretation. Retain controls, excluded alternative causes and superseded harness failures in counterevidence when useful; their presence alone is not a veto. Neither approval nor independent scope can erase a necessary premise. Cite exact target CheckRun IDs and zero-based parsed indices; associate separate producer receipts with joint_history. The controller checks identity and answers, not arbitrary semantic sufficiency.

Changed requirement, applicability, harness, observation or comparison needs a successor with the actual difference and its own execution. An executed probe already containing the same fixed requirement/comparison needs no repeat merely for a new label. Correcting an interpretation of unchanged inputs may reuse existing sources/receipts without a gratuitous edit or rerun; preserve the previous opinion and counterevidence.

Build/setup failure, cancellation and timeout cannot become completed evidence. The narrow exception is a completed violating witness followed by independent test failure: cite its exact receipt, comparison/witness indices, retained output range and fixed harness range, explaining why completion and sampling do not depend on the failure. Other nonzero exits remain unresolved. Preserve concrete limits without repeating generic caveats.
