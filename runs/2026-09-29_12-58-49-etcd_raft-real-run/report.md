- **共识形成与推进**：RawNode.Propose creates MsgProp; Node.run serializes the equivalent state-machine work, while RawNode callers must serialize their own calls. A follower forwards only to a known leader; a candidate drops it. The leader checks membership, transfer and quota, assigns current term/index, appends unstable entries and broadcasts MsgApp anchored by predecessor index/term. The follower checks term and predecessor, rejects conflicts or appends a suffix without overwriting committed history, and queues MsgAppResp. send defers append/election support, including self support, through the Ready storage boundary. After legal caller persistence and delivery, leader response handling records sender Match. The active voter quorum aggregates these values (both majorities in joint configuration); maybeCommit additionally requires a current-term entry beyond committed. Commit is recorded in raftLog and HardState and propagated by append or heartbeat, whose commit is capped by recipient Match. Ready exposes committed application work; async application is additionally bounded by local stabilization. Actual client reply is outside this library and Propose success does not guarantee commitment.
  行为：[B_propose](audit-spec/v3.json): MsgProp from local API or forwarding peer；[B_append_receive](audit-spec/v3.json): MsgApp delivery；[B_defer_support](audit-spec/v3.json): Election or append reply queued; Ready accepted; Advance or local storage response delivered；[B_match](audit-spec/v3.json): Non-rejected MsgAppResp after Step term handling；[B_commit](audit-spec/v3.json): New append acknowledgment or configuration activation invokes maybeCommit；[B_apply](audit-spec/v3.json): Ready delivery and Advance or MsgStorageApplyResp。

Campaign requires a voter with no pending snapshot and no unapplied committed configuration change.
