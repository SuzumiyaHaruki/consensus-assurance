# Consensus investigation

Use two complementary investigation axes to identify correctness relationships:

- **Decision formation and progress:** how eligible support justifies a decision, and how accepted duties reach completion, cancellation or transfer.
- **Context validity and authority transitions:** where that support remains applicable, and how authority is acquired, transferred or lost as context changes.

Trace these relationships through five supporting mechanisms: **recovery and resynchronization** restore eligible participation; **configuration and participant eligibility** determine whose support counts; **decision application and consumption** expose ordering, identity and effects; **history and persistence** retain or reconstruct decision support; **client contracts and completion** supply obligations, invocation intervals and observable endpoints. These mechanisms can directly determine safety; they are not lesser findings, disjoint categories or ordered stages. Asynchronous work can cross both axes and all mechanisms. Broad orientation and local traces refine one another; a sourced question or probe needs no global map, principal Fact or settled requirement. Optional investigation_focus gives reading preferences, not required labels or coverage obligations.

A Behavior describes actual conditional execution: guards, ordering, state changes, messages, returns and completion. Preserve materially different failure, visibility, ownership, persistence and asynchronous paths. A Fact states what those behaviors establish for an identified object and context under explicit conditions. Distinguish required behavior, source assertions and individual observations; neither vacuous True nor an expectation copied from documentation is a useful Fact.

Start from a concrete decision or pending duty: what supports it, who produces and consumes that support, and which permitted event could change those conditions? Trace admission, serialization, reset and completion to see whether the decision remains justified or the duty survives the change. Omitted work can reveal a lost duty without a wrong decision. A provisional hypothesis and discriminator identify the predicted difference and which source or observation would change the explanation. Missing normative support can itself be investigated. Source-explained negatives need equal care; use evidence-review for responsibilities and observations. Reading, construction and repair can share a turn; source reasoning can settle a suspicion without testing.

Promote Behavior/Fact knowledge when reused or needing durable correction, with sources, observations and conditions. Record actual uses by exact revision and relied-on assertion, not shared vocabulary. Revise a changed premise under its existing id. Inspect recorded direct and derived uses, including older revisions, and identify affected investigations, experiments or interpretations in affected_uses with changed_premise. Preserve independent support, unrelated uses, old revisions and receipts. A newly discovered dependency can also be challenged directly through a necessary gap in the affected investigation; no reconstructed knowledge graph is required. Unchanged receipts can support a corrected interpretation.

After a local answer, pause or unchanged update, revisit the investigation axes with the revised understanding and trace relevant supporting mechanisms. Answering all registered questions does not exhaust discovery: examine conditions the current explanation depends on, paths maintaining them and permitted changes that could defeat them, or pursue an independent sourced lead. Coverage suggests locations, not defect hypotheses; reading all files does not exhaust their relationships. Choose a low-cost read or observation that could change the conclusion. A missing legal producer or history calls for that basis, not an estimate of symptom frequency. Keep variants of the same cause and responsibility together; local answers do not settle parents, and pause conditions still apply. Use actual allocation: timeouts are ceilings, not cost estimates or a closing phase. With little time, interpret a saved receipt, correct an account, trace source or leave an unfinished discriminator. No forced alternation, tests, knowledge-use quotas, invented suspicions or repeated closing handoffs.

# Execution-grounded interpretation

Ground conclusions in applicable responsibilities, necessary history and actual contradictable endpoints. A comparison evaluates its declared predicate: holds means satisfied in applicable observations, violated means contradicted by a valid witness, unknown means undecided. A successful anomaly prediction can return holds while supporting a defect explanation. Exploration need not first settle a correctness duty or invert its prediction. Keep predicate meaning and the model's scoped defect_claim separate: execution success, comparison results and structural acceptance do not establish a defect or independent human verification. Review sources, fixed inputs and receipts, not approval votes.

Distinguish guarantees from recommendations, probabilistic descriptions, implementation behavior and individual test expectations; names, guards or a TODO do not establish a universal contract. When responsibility is disputed, reread the full defining contract context, distinguishing caller duties, implementation guarantees and observed branches. Identify who establishes a necessary premise: the caller, environment or target's producer. Prohibited external use alone does not establish a defect; an internal producer violating its consumer's premise under permitted use remains a target responsibility. Prefer normal initialization and actual producers for protocol-dependent claims, using constructors, test facilities, logical ticks, memory transports and storage to control permitted events. Multiple real instances in one process can suffice. Direct handlers and supplied state remain useful exploration: replace consequential supplied premises with real prefixes, find enclosing protections, justify an independently applicable local contract, or retain the gap. Another consumer of the same invented state is not reachability progress.

Reused prefixes/checkpoints must preserve relevant identity, membership, ordering, persistence and pending work. Disk bytes do not recreate replies, workers or futures; matching numbers do not make fragments one compatible history. Justify particular omissions, executing the prefix when easier. Match fault assumptions to events: a failing-write window is not one failed call, nor an allowed store error corruption of an acknowledged durable write. Initialization does not legalize later inputs, and API acceptance does not prove caller permission. A local endpoint cannot waive necessary calling/history conditions or settle broader consequences.

Trace decisive observations from the actual runtime object through callbacks or returns to sampling and completion. Check identity, actual returned values, operands, predicate and operation order against harness and raw output, not scenario names. When a decisive proxy is ambiguous or observations conflict, inspect their computation and sampling before rerunning or expanding the scenario. Prefer existing independent observations that distinguish concrete rival explanations; source and retained output may suffice. Derived booleans need inspectable operands; constants are not observed triggers. Before/after samples can miss intermediate transitions; zero detections alone establish neither absence nor an occurrence-rate bound. Resolve consequential sampling ambiguity through synchronization, ownership or a check that the observer detects the relevant transition. Log entries and quorum counters are not FSM effects; calls are not completions. Missing data, including an unobserved implication antecedent, remains unknown: retain the result event, independent identity and sampling failure reason, but omit unobserved compared fields rather than defaulting them to false, zero or an empty string. JSON null is a value, not a missing-field marker. Preserve the actual endpoint and timing: output timestamps need not mark the underlying event.

A bounded progress defect can be supported by a legal trigger and actual authority/producer prefix, repeated nonprogress under stated fair/normal opportunities, and a source-supported retry/reject/reset cycle with no enabled repair under those conditions. State the actual interval, mechanism and environmental/fault assumptions. A short wait, artificial timeout or isolated stalled goroutine without this basis is insufficient; a supported scoped defect needs neither infinite execution nor proof against every future repair. Controls should distinguish concrete competing causes with consistent sampling and comparison where practical. If the result persists without its proposed cause, investigate the harness and predicate. No fixed control quota is required; decisive observations or sourced explanations can suffice. Never modify production logic to manufacture evidence.

Identity must be independent of compared values; do not identify responses by potentially corrupted context or filter applicability on expected results. Each comparison requires its admission and referenced prerequisite chains, not alternative scenario admissions. Preserve missing completions, contradictory same-operation observations and parse loss. A complete witness may survive an independent unfinished scenario, never missing same-witness history.

Let consequential counterevidence change the original question's explanation. Determine whether it defeats a necessary premise, limits the consequence, or excludes a particular trigger or competing cause. A calling restriction or protection defeating the premise requires correction, narrowing or refutation; listing it as a limitation does not preserve the claim. Retention needs sourced reasons it does not apply; narrowing needs its own applicable duty. Otherwise retain an unresolved claim and investigate that premise rather than reproducing the disputed symptom. A successful path only settles what it establishes; retain and investigate concrete unobserved failure, producer or completion paths. Keep controls, excluded causes and superseded failures. Necessary gaps affect applicability, legal history, observation validity or cause and remain until specifically answered; broader impact can be separate when the local duty stands independently. Cite exact target CheckRun IDs and zero-based parsed indices, linking separate producer receipts with joint_history. The controller validates recorded identity and answers, not arbitrary semantic sufficiency.

Interpretation owns current findings, counterevidence and limits, including source-only accounts before execution. Its latest version covers the whole question. Compare a revised account with the prior conditions and counterevidence; explain what new evidence or source understanding supports the actual change, retaining still-applicable findings and boundaries with references. Correct interpretations that depended on invalid sampling while preserving independent observations. Stronger consequences need their own support, not just repeated observations. Reuse unchanged receipts to correct an explanation without recopying history or rerunning for labels; exploration without a fixed requirement can support later sourced interpretation while its original basis remains intact. Changed requirement, applicability, harness, observation or comparison needs a successor with the actual difference and fresh execution. Read existing outputs first; repeats can examine reproducibility or schedule variation, but cannot establish that the measurement means what its label claims.

Build/setup failure, cancellation and timeout cannot become completed evidence. A completed violating witness followed by independent test failure is a narrow exception: cite the receipt, comparison/witness indices, output and harness ranges, explaining why completion and sampling do not depend on the failure. Other failed receipts can support explained/unresolved/conditional accounts without claiming a completed comparison. Do not delete assertions or rerun unchanged inputs merely to obtain success. Preserve concrete limits without repetitive generic caveats.

# One file-backed update

Read research.json for source/draft paths, questions, actual results and locators, necessary gaps, changed knowledge, backend/build inputs and allocation. Detailed history lives in state.json and referenced receipts. Start with the small update example; consult only needed $defs in submission.schema.json, not the whole schema.

Write the update as JSON in draft. The final response is a separate JSON object: {"submission":"update.json","summary":"Short summary"}, using the draft-relative path, with no Markdown or trailing prose. Optional validation.command reports validity and, for valid updates, no_change and execution_requested against saved state; it does not accept, execute or reserve capacity. Validity does not establish semantic correctness. Acceptance reads retained bytes. Rejected whole drafts and diagnostics return for ordinary repair in this session; editable drafts never override receipts.

The update file requires rationale; sources, investigations, knowledge, gaps, interpretations, experiment, repeat_experiment_id and stop are optional. Existing records use explicit IDs. Unique @names may refer to new questions/knowledge within a batch. The controller assigns IDs, revisions and comparison indices. Source IDs identify acquired file/ranges; do not create aliases merely to count handoffs.

- investigations: create a sourced question or update specified fields of id. Hypothesis/discriminator may be provisional; update the same id when evidence changes them. parent_id links a child without closing its parent. paused needs resume_conditions. Current counterevidence and limitations belong only to Interpretation, including source-only accounts.
- knowledge: behavior/fact statement, conditions and sources, optionally execution indices and direct knowledge uses. References specify exact entry_id/revision and optionally the assertion used. Correct an existing id using changed_premise; affected_uses identifies affected terminal investigations/experiments/interpretations through recorded direct or derived uses, including older revisions, not every reachable use automatically. Supplement a correction's affected uses without gratuitous body changes. Revise derived knowledge under its own id where needed. Old bases remain available.
- gaps: necessary question under investigation_id; optional experiment_id limits it to that experiment and successors. Put broader directions in discriminator or Interpretation.limitations. Interpretation.answers maps exact gap IDs to {"explanation":"Specific sourced/observed basis", "resolved":false}. Set resolved=true only when that basis resolves the gap; false records partial progress or reopens it. Omitted IDs retain earlier answers. Identical updates do not duplicate gaps.
- interpretations: investigation_id, optional experiment_id, source_ids, rationale and conclusion (explained, conditional, violated, holds, unresolved) describes the comparison/evidence interpretation, not a defect verdict. Required defect_claim is asserted (a sourced defect under stated valid conditions), refuted (the suspicion is defeated), unresolved (a necessary uncertainty remains), or null (no current defect judgment). There is no inheritance. Follow evidence-review for the current account, counterevidence and limitations. executions maps target receipt IDs to decisive parsed indices; an experiment interpretation needs its own receipt. Separate producer receipts need joint_history. Explained/unresolved/conditional accounts may retain failed receipts and observed output without claiming a completed comparison. Preserve explicit knowledge uses.
- experiment: one invocation with investigation_id, harness_path, optional files (workspace destination → draft path), source_ids, scope and prefix, including supplied assumptions. requirement/responsibility and comparisons are optional; requirements need verified anchors, which do not prove source meaning. A comparison owns event, identity_fields, admission_alias, assertion and optional antecedent/applicability_conditions. Only its admission and referenced aliases plus their transitive dependencies are required; link necessary history explicitly, not by array order. For alternative setup events mode=A/B, use separate admissions with mode conditions and independent result applicability; never filter on the measured result. Cases/controls can share execution. Include the current discriminator's needed cases, helpers and controls; do not accumulate unrelated historical tests. Fixed artifacts retain earlier scenarios, and explicit repeat remains available. Changed basis uses previous_id/revision_reason and fresh execution.
- repeat_experiment_id: execute an existing fixed experiment once more from its retained inputs in a clean copy. Explain the reproduction question in rationale. Mutually exclusive with experiment and stop; consumes one execution and the existing time budget. The new receipt belongs to the same fixed experiment; cite its exact CheckRun ID. Ordinary unchanged submissions remain deduplicated.
- stop: bounded_completed only for a preconfigured finite directed task with no necessary unfinished work. Cancellation, service refusal, permission loss and exhausted resources are controller boundaries, not model declarations.

Go execution_package selects one captured local package; Rust selects a crate and tests/<basename>.rs; Python has no package override. Follow backend instructions for protected source, dependencies, locks, features, support and caches. Exploration and specified checks use identical fixed-input isolation. Agent shell networking is disabled; executor communication is confined to its private namespace.

Exact unchanged updates receive no_change without duplicate revisions or automatic stopping. The reselection view provides bounded outcomes, corrected knowledge and locators for continued inquiry; full history remains available. Turn allocation and CheckRun establish timing; preflight elapsed time is a checkpoint. Expiry needs no closing submission.
