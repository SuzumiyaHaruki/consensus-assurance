# Consensus investigation

Use consensus formation/progress and context/authority transitions as complementary lines: how decisions become justified and accepted duties reach completion, cancellation or transfer, and how their basis survives authority acquisition, transfer or loss. Membership, persistence, recovery, asynchronous work and client contracts connect them. Other Activities support investigation, not mandatory labels or stages. Broad orientation and local traces refine one another; a sourced question or probe needs no global map, principal Fact or settled requirement.

A Behavior describes actual conditional execution: guards, ordering, state changes, messages, returns and completion. Preserve materially different failure, visibility, ownership, persistence and asynchronous paths. A Fact states what those behaviors establish for an identified object and context under explicit conditions. Distinguish required behavior, source assertions and individual observations; neither vacuous True nor an expectation copied from documentation is a useful Fact.

Start from a concrete decision or pending duty and trace its producer, consumer and a real event that may change their shared conditions. A provisional hypothesis and discriminator can identify what the current explanation predicts and which source or observation would change it. Trace admission, serialization, reset and completion; omitted work can reveal a lost duty without a wrong decision. Names, guards, asymmetry and existing tests do not prove eligibility, freshness or same-operation association. Missing normative support can itself be investigated; current behavior or a TODO does not establish a universal contract. Source-explained negatives need equal care. Reading, construction and repair can share a turn, and source reasoning can settle a suspicion without testing. Use evidence-review for applicable responsibilities and observed endpoints, not an exhaustive checklist or private chain of thought.

Promote Behavior/Fact knowledge when reused or needing durable correction, with sources, observations and conditions. Record the exact entry revision and relied-on assertion for actual uses, not shared vocabulary. When evidence changes a saved premise, revise its existing id; a new Fact cannot substitute for correction. Inspect recorded direct and derived uses, including older revisions, and identify affected terminal investigations, experiments or interpretations in affected_uses with changed_premise. Reliance is not automatic invalidity: preserve independent support, unrelated uses, old revisions and receipts. Answer challenged premises in their original investigation; unchanged receipts can support a corrected interpretation.

After a local answer, pause or unchanged update, return to A1/A2 with the revised understanding. Which sourced relationship or condition could change the current explanation? A protection or recovery path may expose a partial failure, a different producer, or queued work after authority change. Coverage suggests locations, not independent defect questions or guarantees; reading all files does not exhaust their relationships. Investigate tractable producer, prefix, schedule and endpoint gaps. Keep one investigation for variants of the same cause and responsibility; refutation is useful, local answers do not settle parents, and pause conditions still apply. Choose useful work using actual allocation; timeouts are ceilings, not predicted costs or a closing phase. With little time, a source trace or honest unfinished discriminator can suffice. No forced alternation, tests, knowledge-use quotas, invented suspicions or repeated closing handoffs.

# Execution-grounded interpretation

Ground conclusions in applicable responsibilities, necessary history and actual contradictable endpoints. A comparison evaluates its declared predicate: holds means satisfied in applicable observations, violated means contradicted by a valid witness, unknown means undecided. A successful anomaly prediction can return holds while supporting a defect explanation. Exploration need not first settle a correctness duty or invert its prediction. Keep predicate meaning and the model's scoped defect_claim separate: execution success, comparison results and structural acceptance do not establish a defect or independent human verification. Review sources, fixed inputs and receipts, not approval votes.

Identify who establishes a necessary premise: the external caller, environment or target's producer. Prohibited external use alone does not establish a defect; an internal producer violating its consumer's premise under permitted use remains a target responsibility. Prefer normal initialization and actual producers for protocol-dependent claims, controlling permitted events with constructors, test facilities, logical ticks, memory transports and storage. Multiple real instances in one process can suffice. Direct handlers and supplied state remain useful exploration: replace consequential supplied premises with real prefixes, find enclosing protections, justify an independently applicable local contract, or retain the gap. Another consumer of the same invented state is not reachability progress.

Reused prefixes/checkpoints must preserve relevant identity, membership, ordering, persistence and pending work. Disk bytes do not recreate replies, workers or futures; matching numbers do not make fragments one compatible history. Justify particular omissions, executing the prefix when easier. Match fault assumptions to events: a failing-write window is not one failed call, nor an allowed store error corruption of an acknowledged durable write. Initialization does not legalize later inputs, and API acceptance does not prove caller permission. A local endpoint cannot waive necessary calling/history conditions or settle broader consequences.

Trace decisive observations from the actual runtime object through callbacks or returns to sampling and completion. Check identity, operands, predicate and operation order against harness and raw output, not scenario names. Before/after samples cannot exclude intermediate transitions. Resolve consequential zeroes, reversals or timing ambiguity through synchronization or justified ownership. Derived booleans need inspectable operands and computation; scenario constants are not observed triggers. Log entries and quorum counters are not FSM effects; calls are not completions. Missing data, including an unobserved implication antecedent, remains unknown. Summaries must preserve the actual endpoint and timing: output or later sampling timestamps need not mark the underlying event.

A bounded progress defect can be supported by a legal trigger and actual authority/producer prefix, repeated nonprogress under stated fair/normal opportunities, and a source-supported retry/reject/reset cycle with no enabled repair under those conditions. State the actual interval, mechanism and environmental/fault assumptions. A short wait, artificial timeout or isolated stalled goroutine without this basis is insufficient; a supported scoped defect needs neither infinite execution nor proof against every future repair. Controls should distinguish concrete competing causes with consistent sampling and comparison where practical. If the result persists without its proposed cause, investigate the harness and predicate. No fixed control quota is required; decisive observations or sourced explanations can suffice. Never modify production logic to manufacture evidence.

Identity must be independent of compared values; do not identify responses by potentially corrupted context or filter applicability on expected results. Each comparison requires its admission and referenced prerequisite chains, not alternative scenario admissions. Preserve missing completions, contradictory same-operation observations and parse loss. A complete witness may survive an independent unfinished scenario, never missing same-witness history.

Let consequential counterevidence change the original question's explanation. Determine whether it defeats a necessary premise, limits the consequence, or excludes a particular trigger or competing cause. A calling restriction or protection defeating the premise requires correction, narrowing or refutation; listing it as a limitation does not preserve the claim. Retention needs sourced reasons it does not apply; narrowing needs its own applicable duty. Otherwise retain an unresolved claim and investigate that premise rather than reproducing the disputed symptom. A successful path only settles what it establishes; retain and investigate concrete unobserved failure, producer or completion paths. Keep controls, excluded causes and superseded failures. Necessary gaps affect applicability, legal history, observation validity or cause and remain until specifically answered; broader impact can be separate when the local duty stands independently. Cite exact target CheckRun IDs and zero-based parsed indices, linking separate producer receipts with joint_history. The controller validates recorded identity and answers, not arbitrary semantic sufficiency.

Changed requirement, applicability, harness, observation or comparison needs a successor with the actual difference and fresh execution. An exploratory receipt without a fixed requirement can support later sourced interpretation; preserve its original basis and limitations. Reuse unchanged receipts to correct, narrow or refute an explanation without recopying history or rerunning to change labels. Read existing outputs first; repeat execution to distinguish remaining explanations or examine reproducibility/schedule variation, not to turn repetition into a universal guarantee.

Build/setup failure, cancellation and timeout cannot become completed evidence. A completed violating witness followed by independent test failure is a narrow exception: cite the receipt, comparison/witness indices, output and harness ranges, explaining why completion and sampling do not depend on the failure. Other failed receipts can support explained/unresolved/conditional accounts without claiming a completed comparison. Do not delete assertions or rerun unchanged inputs merely to obtain success. Preserve concrete limits without repetitive generic caveats.

# One file-backed update

Read research.json for source/draft paths, questions, actual results and locators, necessary gaps, changed knowledge, backend/build inputs and allocation. Detailed history lives in state.json and referenced receipts. Start with the small update example; consult only needed $defs in submission.schema.json, not the whole schema.

Write the update as JSON in draft. The final response is a separate JSON object: {"submission":"update.json","summary":"Short summary"}, using the draft-relative path, with no Markdown or trailing prose. Optional validation.command checks file bytes read-only and reserves no capacity. Acceptance reads retained bytes. Rejected whole drafts and diagnostics return for ordinary repair in this session; editable drafts never override receipts.

The update file requires rationale; sources, investigations, knowledge, gaps, interpretations, experiment, repeat_experiment_id and stop are optional. Existing records use explicit IDs. Unique @names may refer to new questions/knowledge within a batch. The controller assigns IDs, revisions and comparison indices. Source IDs identify acquired file/ranges; do not create aliases merely to count handoffs.

- investigations: create a sourced question or update specified fields of id. Hypothesis/discriminator may be provisional; update the same id when new evidence changes them. parent_id links a child without closing its parent. paused needs resume_conditions; explained/closed retain unresolved boundaries and grounded interpretations.
- knowledge: behavior/fact statement, conditions and sources, optionally execution indices and direct knowledge uses. References specify exact entry_id/revision and optionally the assertion used. Correct an existing id using changed_premise; affected_uses identifies affected terminal investigations/experiments/interpretations through recorded direct or derived uses, including older revisions, not every reachable use automatically. Supplement a correction's affected uses without gratuitous body changes. Revise derived knowledge under its own id where needed. Old bases remain available.
- gaps: necessary question under investigation_id; optional experiment_id limits it to that experiment and successors. Put broader directions in discriminator or limitations. Interpretation.answers maps exact gap IDs to sourced/observed explanations; null explicitly withdraws a prior answer, with the reason in rationale. Omitted IDs retain earlier answers; a new explanation can answer again. Identical updates do not duplicate gaps.
- interpretations: investigation_id, optional experiment_id, source_ids, rationale and conclusion (explained, conditional, violated, holds, unresolved) describes the comparison/evidence interpretation, not a defect verdict. Optional defect_claim is asserted (a sourced correctness-defect claim in stated valid conditions), refuted (the suspicion is defeated), or unresolved (a necessary uncertainty remains). The latest interpretation is the whole question's current account, not the latest execution's summary. Retain still-applicable findings, counterevidence and limits through concise rationale and receipt references; revise what new evidence changes without recopying history. Omitted defect_claim means no explicit current claim, not inheritance. Human verification is separate. executions maps target receipt IDs to decisive parsed indices; an experiment interpretation needs its own receipt. Separate producer receipts need joint_history. Explained/unresolved/conditional accounts may retain failed receipts and observed output without claiming a completed comparison. Preserve explicit knowledge uses.
- experiment: one invocation with investigation_id, harness_path, optional files (workspace destination → draft path), source_ids, scope and prefix, including supplied assumptions. requirement/responsibility and comparisons are optional; requirements need verified anchors, which do not prove source meaning. A comparison owns event, identity_fields, admission_alias, assertion and optional antecedent/applicability_conditions. Only its admission and referenced aliases plus their transitive dependencies are required; link necessary history explicitly, not by array order. For alternative setup events mode=A/B, use separate admissions with mode conditions and independent result applicability; never filter on the measured result. Cases/controls can share execution. Changed basis uses previous_id/revision_reason and fresh execution.
- repeat_experiment_id: execute an existing fixed experiment once more from its retained inputs in a clean copy. Explain the reproduction question in rationale. Mutually exclusive with experiment and stop; consumes one execution and the existing time budget. The new receipt belongs to the same fixed experiment; cite its exact CheckRun ID. Ordinary unchanged submissions remain deduplicated.
- stop: bounded_completed only for a preconfigured finite directed task with no necessary unfinished work. Cancellation, service refusal, permission loss and exhausted resources are controller boundaries, not model declarations.

Go execution_package selects one captured local package; Rust selects a crate and tests/<basename>.rs; Python has no package override. Follow backend instructions for protected source, dependencies, locks, features, support and caches. Exploration and specified checks use identical fixed-input isolation. Agent shell networking is disabled; executor communication is confined to its private namespace.

Exact unchanged updates receive no_change without duplicate revisions or automatic stopping. The reselection view provides bounded outcomes, corrected knowledge and locators for continued inquiry; full history remains available. Turn allocation and CheckRun establish timing; preflight elapsed time is a checkpoint. Expiry needs no closing submission.
