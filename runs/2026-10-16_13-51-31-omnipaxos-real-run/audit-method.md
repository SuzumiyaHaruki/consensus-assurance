# Consensus investigation

Use two complementary investigation axes to identify correctness relationships:

- **Decision formation and progress:** how eligible support justifies a decision, and how accepted duties reach completion, cancellation or transfer.
- **Context validity and authority transitions:** where that support remains applicable, and how authority is acquired, transferred or lost as context changes.

Trace these relationships through five supporting mechanisms: **recovery and resynchronization** restore eligible participation; **configuration and participant eligibility** determine whose support counts; **decision application and consumption** expose ordering, identity and effects; **history and persistence** retain or reconstruct decision support; **client contracts and completion** supply obligations, invocation intervals and observable endpoints. These mechanisms can directly determine safety; they are not lesser findings, disjoint categories or ordered stages. Asynchronous work can cross both axes and all mechanisms. Broad orientation and local traces refine one another; a sourced question or probe needs no global map, principal Fact or settled requirement. Optional investigation_focus gives reading preferences, not required labels or coverage obligations.

A Behavior describes actual conditional execution: guards, ordering, state changes, messages, returns and completion. Preserve materially different failure, visibility, ownership, persistence and asynchronous paths. A Fact states what those behaviors establish for an identified object and context under explicit conditions. Distinguish required behavior, source assertions and individual observations; neither vacuous True nor an expectation copied from documentation is a useful Fact.

Start from a concrete decision or pending duty: what supports it, who produces and consumes that support, and which permitted event could change those conditions? Trace admission, serialization, reset and completion to see whether the decision remains justified or the duty survives the change. Omitted work can reveal a lost duty without a wrong decision. A provisional hypothesis predicts a difference; the discriminator identifies information that could still change the judgment. Completed comparisons belong in Interpretation; answered questions need no invented next discriminator. Missing normative support can itself be investigated. Source-explained negatives need equal care; use evidence-review for responsibilities and observations. Reading, construction and repair can share a turn; source reasoning can settle a suspicion without testing.

Promote Behavior/Fact knowledge when reused or needing durable correction, with sources, observations and conditions. Record actual uses by exact revision and relied-on assertion, not shared vocabulary. Revise a changed premise under its existing id. Inspect recorded direct and derived uses, including older revisions, and identify affected investigations, experiments or interpretations in affected_uses with changed_premise. Preserve independent support, unrelated uses, old revisions and receipts. A newly discovered dependency can also be challenged directly through a necessary gap in the affected investigation; no reconstructed knowledge graph is required. Unchanged receipts can support a corrected interpretation.

After a local answer, pause or unchanged update, undertake the next judgment-changing action from the revised understanding and captured sources. Revisit the axes and supporting mechanisms; the latest execution does not determine the next topic. Registered questions do not bound discovery, and uncited receipts are not a checklist to clear. Examine a necessary premise, the paths maintaining it and permitted changes that could defeat it, or pursue an independent sourced relationship. Prefer a low-cost read or observation addressing a consequential uncertainty; no separate selection plan is required. Coverage suggests locations, not hypotheses; reading all files does not exhaust their relationships. Missing legal producers or history call for that basis, not more symptom variants. Keep variants of the same cause and responsibility together; local answers do not settle parents, and pause conditions still apply. Use actual allocation: timeouts are ceilings, not cost estimates or a closing phase. With little time, interpret a relevant saved receipt, correct an account, trace source or leave an unfinished discriminator. No forced alternation, tests, knowledge-use quotas, invented suspicions or repeated closing handoffs.

# Execution-grounded interpretation

Ground conclusions in applicable responsibilities, necessary history and actual contradictable endpoints. A comparison evaluates its declared predicate: holds means satisfied in applicable observations, violated means contradicted by a valid witness, unknown means undecided. A successful anomaly prediction can return holds while supporting a defect explanation. Exploration need not first settle a correctness duty or invert its prediction. Keep predicate meaning and the model's scoped defect_claim separate: execution success, comparison results and structural acceptance do not establish a defect or independent human verification. Review sources, fixed inputs and receipts, not approval votes.

Distinguish guarantees from recommendations, probabilistic descriptions, implementation behavior and individual test expectations; names, guards or a TODO do not establish a universal contract. When responsibility is disputed, reread the defining contract, distinguishing caller duties, guarantees and observed branches. Identify who establishes a necessary premise: caller, environment or target producer. Prohibited external use alone does not establish a defect; an internal producer violating its consumer's premise under permitted use remains a target responsibility. Trace the decisive input's permitted origin: real surrounding nodes, valid fields and API acceptance do not establish it. Controlled delivery and processing must preserve the target fault model and actual transport, ordering, cancellation and RPC lifecycle semantics. Constructors, test facilities, logical ticks, memory transports and storage can suffice, including multiple real instances in one process. Direct handlers and supplied state remain useful exploration: replace consequential supplied premises with real prefixes, find enclosing protections, justify an independently applicable local contract, or retain the gap. Another consumer of the same invented state is not reachability progress.

Reused prefixes/checkpoints must preserve relevant identity, membership, ordering, persistence and pending work, including caller duties omitted by reused helpers. Progress markers do not establish completion of those duties. Disk bytes do not recreate replies, workers or futures; matching numbers do not make fragments one compatible history. Replaying or shrinking a retained action prefix can locate the first conflict between construction and source explanations; preserve relevant preconditions, since a seed alone may not preserve action choices. Match fault assumptions to events: a failing-write window is not one failed call, nor an allowed store error corruption of an acknowledged durable write. A local endpoint cannot waive necessary calling/history conditions or settle broader consequences.

For a new or strengthened consequence, scope or exclusion, check the added inference and necessary premise against fixed code, actual observations and concrete permitted explanations. When attributing an observed change to an operation, align the same object's state before the operation and its observed change afterward; an outcome already present does not establish that operation's effect. Trace decisive values through producers, consumers, callbacks, returns, sampling and completion; check identity, operands, predicate, order and invocation/completion intervals. Scenario labels do not establish these relations; adjacent statements do not establish atomicity. Separate source-supported unexecuted paths from this execution's measurements. When proxies are ambiguous or observations conflict, inspect computation and sampling before expanding or rerunning. Preserve distinctions the judgment needs: target not found versus a false property, return representation versus application content. Resolve ambiguity using source, existing observations or a discriminating control as needed, without a control quota. Negative measurements must distinguish the hypothesis under stated conditions. Derived booleans need inspectable operands; constants are not observed triggers. Before/after samples can miss intermediate transitions; zero detections establish neither absence nor a rate bound. Address consequential sampling ambiguity through synchronization, ownership or checking that the observer detects the relevant transition. Log entries and quorum counters are not FSM effects; calls are not completions. Missing data, including an unobserved antecedent, stays unknown: retain the result event, independent identity and sampling failure reason, omitting unobserved compared fields rather than defaulting to false, zero or an empty string. JSON null is a value, not a missing-field marker. Preserve endpoint timing; output timestamps need not mark the underlying event.

A bounded progress defect can be supported by a legal trigger and actual authority/producer prefix, repeated nonprogress under stated fair/normal opportunities, and a source-supported retry/reject/reset cycle with no enabled repair under those conditions. State the actual interval, mechanism and environmental/fault assumptions. A short wait, artificial timeout or isolated stalled goroutine without this basis is insufficient; a supported scoped defect needs neither infinite execution nor proof against every future repair. When using an intervention to exclude a competing cause, establish that it actually changed or isolated that cause, rather than only a proxy parameter. Keep sampling and comparison consistent where relevant; persistence after removing the proposed cause calls its explanation into question. Never modify production logic to manufacture evidence.

Identity must be independent of compared values; do not identify responses by potentially corrupted context or filter applicability on expected results. Each comparison requires its admission and referenced prerequisite chains, not alternative scenario admissions. Preserve missing completions, contradictory same-operation observations and parse loss. A complete witness may survive an independent unfinished scenario, never missing same-witness history.

Let consequential counterevidence change the original question's explanation. Determine whether it defeats a necessary premise, limits the consequence, or excludes only one trigger or competing cause; the last does not by itself settle the original question or its parent. A calling restriction or protection defeating the premise requires correction, narrowing or refutation; listing it as a limitation does not preserve the claim. Retention needs sourced reasons it does not apply; narrowing needs its own applicable duty. Otherwise retain an unresolved claim and investigate that premise rather than reproducing the disputed symptom. A successful path only settles what it establishes; retain and investigate concrete unobserved failure, producer or completion paths. Keep controls, excluded causes and superseded failures. Necessary gaps affect applicability, legal history, observation validity or cause and remain until specifically answered; broader impact can be separate when the local duty stands independently. Cite exact target CheckRun IDs and zero-based parsed indices, linking separate producer receipts with joint_history. The controller validates recorded identity and answers, not arbitrary semantic sufficiency.

Interpretation owns the whole question's current explanation, counterevidence and limits, including before execution. Start with the present conclusion and basis; AuditSubmission.rationale explains this update's change. Preserve superseded accounts in history and still-applicable findings, counterevidence and boundaries in the current account. Merged mechanisms retain their supporting sources, receipts and conditions; do not combine measurements across executions into an unsupported account. Current prose is not evidence: check retained claims against their original basis and explicitly withdraw invalid support. Old and new knowledge references locate possible revision sites, not automatic invalidation; correct affected premises and uses, preserving independent observations. Withdrawing a witness does not itself refute the question or require repairing every abandoned test. Correct interpretations of unchanged evidence without rerunning; exploration without a fixed requirement can support later sourced interpretation while retaining its original basis. Changed requirement, applicability, harness, observation or comparison needs a successor recording the actual difference and fresh execution. Repeats support reproducibility under exercised conditions, not schedule independence or measurement validity.

Build/setup failure, cancellation and timeout cannot become completed evidence. A completed violating witness alongside independent test failure is a narrow exception: cite the receipt, comparison/witness indices, output and harness ranges, explaining why its premises, completion and sampling do not depend on the failure; test names or execution order alone do not establish independence. Other failed receipts can support explained/unresolved/conditional accounts without claiming a completed comparison. Do not delete assertions or rerun unchanged inputs merely to obtain success. Preserve concrete limits without repetitive generic caveats.

# One file-backed update

Use research.json as current navigation for source/draft paths, questions, results, necessary gaps, changed knowledge, build inputs and allocation. Explicitly abbreviated previews are not complete records; state.json and referenced artifacts retain the full records and history. Start with the small update example; consult only needed $defs in submission.schema.json, not the whole schema.

Write the update as JSON in draft. The final response is a separate JSON object: {"submission":"update.json","summary":"Short summary"}, using the draft-relative path, with no Markdown or trailing prose. Summary briefly states this turn's actual change, action and incomplete work; the current account remains in Interpretation. Optional validation.command reports validity and, for valid updates, no_change, execution_requested and derived effects against saved state: record creation, update, interpretation append or unchanged. It does not accept, execute or reserve capacity; effects describe record changes, not semantic correctness or research progress. Acceptance reads retained bytes. Rejected whole drafts and diagnostics return for ordinary repair in this session; editable drafts never override receipts.

The update file requires rationale explaining this update's action and change; Interpretation.rationale holds the current question account, not a prefixed revision log. Sources, investigations, knowledge, gaps, interpretations, experiment, repeat_experiment_id and stop are optional. Existing records use explicit IDs. Unique @names may refer to new questions/knowledge within a batch. The controller assigns IDs, revisions and comparison indices. Source IDs identify acquired file/ranges; do not create aliases merely to count handoffs. For local corrections, read the full current record, copy its fields accepted by the relevant submission schema into draft, and edit with native tools or a short script. Optional preflight effects help inspect the actual changes.

Ordinary unchanged submissions remain deduplicated.
